Signs You Need Emergency Heating Repair in West Jordan

When the temperature starts to drop and the cold winter mountain winds arrive in West Jordan, it’s important to know if your heating issue is a true emergency or a normal repair. Call Sunny Home Services for emergency furnace services if:

  • ✓ There is no heat coming from the furnace: This is especially concerning during a snowstorm or a cold night, when temperatures typically drop into the single digits.
  • ✓ There are strange smells: A rotten-egg smell indicates a gas leak, a burning or smoky smell indicates an electrical failure, and both are extremely dangerous.
  • ✓ There are unusual noises: Any grinding, banging, or hissing is a sign that a crucial part has broken or is about to.
  • ✓ Carbon monoxide alarms: If this alarm goes off, call Sunny Home Services and leave the area.
  • ✓ The system is short cycling: If the heater is turning off and on before completing a full cycle, this needs to be looked at by a Sunny Home Services technician.
  • ✓ There’s ice visible on the heater: During our West Jordan winters, ice can build up when there are issues with the heat exchanger or the venting.

If you’re unsure whether your heating issue qualifies as an emergency, it never hurts to call Sunny Home Services. We’ll determine whether we need to assess the problem immediately or if it can wait until morning.

What to Do Before a Sunny Home Services Technician Arrives

While you wait for one of our team members to arrive, there are a few steps you can take to stay safe and help speed up the emergency service.

1
Step One
Always be safe. If you smell gas or see sparks or smoke coming from the indoor or outdoor unit, shut down the unit at the thermostat and turn off the main breaker. If you suspect a gas leak, evacuate everyone and call Sunny Home Services.
2
Step Two
If there is no immediate safety concern, check your thermostat and see if the batteries need to be replaced. Next, check your electrical panel to see if a breaker tripped. If it has, flip it on. If it trips again, just leave it alone and call us.
3
Step Three
While waiting for the technician to arrive, check your unit’s air filter. These can become clogged with debris and need to be replaced every one to three months.
4
Step Four
To speed up the emergency repair process, clear the area and make sure there is at least three feet of clearance from the unit. Write down the age, make, and model of the unit, and document any of the symptoms you saw or heard before the unit failed.
5
Step Five
Keep all interior doors open to evenly distribute the remaining heat, and keep warm with blankets and cover any exposed piping to prevent freeze bursts.
