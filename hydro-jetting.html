Hydro Jetting in West Jordan, UT

Book Now Call 385.853.8116

If you’ve ever found yourself ankle-deep in water after a shower or struggling to drain the sink after washing dishes, you know how much of a disruption a severe drain clog can be. Sometimes, clogs are too stubborn to clear with normal methods, so the issue may require hydro jetting in West Jordan, UT, from the professionals at Sunny Home Services. This method uses a high-pressure stream of water to blast away obstructions without damaging the surrounding pipes, while also removing any additional buildup from pipe walls.

How To Tell You Need Hydro Jetting in Your West Jordan Home

Not all drain issues require hydro jet drain cleaning. Although the only way to definitively determine the source of your plumbing issue is with a camera inspection of your pipes, there are a few signs our plumbers look for that indicate a tough clog:

  • ✓ Recurring, frequent clogs
  • ✓ Multiple clogs throughout the home
  • ✓ Gurgling sounds from pipes and toilets
  • ✓ Foul sewage odors from drains
  • ✓ Sewage backups

If any of these symptoms sound familiar, there’s a good chance you have a clog deep in your plumbing that requires hydro jetting to clear.

What Hydro Jetting Can Clear

Hydro jetting offers a powerful solution for severe drain clogs in your West Jordan home.

Fat, oil, and grease

Fat, oil, and grease (FOG) can form severe drain clogs, congealing and hardening into extremely stubborn blockages that chemical drain cleaners and other conventional methods can’t touch. Hydro jetting pipes is a great way to completely remove these clogs and scour the pipe walls at the same time to prevent further blockages.

Mineral buildup

The water in West Jordan contains high levels of calcium and magnesium. While not harmful to consume, these minerals can create a range of plumbing problems, including scale buildup on the inside of your pipe walls. The rough surface of these scale clogs also traps other debris, making clogs even worse over time. A sewer drain jet cleaning service can address this issue.

Soap scum

Another side effect of West Jordan’s hard water is soap scum, which happens when the fatty acids in soap react with calcium and magnesium. This binds with body oils, skin cells, hair, and dirt, creating severe blockages over time. Hydro jetting is the most reliable way to clear these clogs.

Hair

Hair can mix with soap scum and other substances to create tough clogs in your bathroom drains. If you’ve been neglecting to catch fallen hair with a drain strainer, there’s a good chance you’ll need to find hydro jet plumbing near you before long.

Sand, silt, and mud

Sometimes, sand, silt, and mud can form tough sludge blockages in drains. Be careful if you’re frequently washing off very dirty belongings in the home, as this can create serious blockages in your drain pipes or further down in the sewer line. Fortunately, hydro jetting is a reliable way to clear a sludge clog.

Food buildup

If you put a lot of food debris down your drains without a garbage disposal, it can easily cause a stubborn clog. If the debris contains a lot of fat or oil, it can make the blockage even worse, requiring hydro jetting to clear it reliably.

Tree roots

Root infiltration into sewage lines is a common issue with fast-growing, water-hungry West Jordan tree species, such as willow, cottonwood, and poplar. The roots of these trees can break through and block underground pipes in search of water. Hydro jetting can clear the blockage, though you may also need trenchless sewer repair to fix any damage.

Paper products and other debris

Paper towels, cotton swabs, wet wipes, and hygiene products can expand and create a severe drain clog, which is why you should always throw these products in the garbage. If you have a paper or cotton debris drain clog, hydro jetting may be used to clear it.

Our Comprehensive Hydro Jetting Services for Sewer Lines in West Jordan, UT

Water jetting a sewage line is an effective way to clear deep clogs. A clogged sewer line affects every drain in the home, so it may be considered a plumbing emergency. A sewer line clog can prevent all drains from functioning, allow harmful sewage gases into your home, or cause a dangerous sewage backup in your drains and fixtures.

Hydro jetting the sewer line is the best way to clear these deep clogs without the need for an invasive sewer line repair. Our team begins with a comprehensive inspection to find the source of the issue, which then allows us to address it at the source.

Sunny Home Services Hydro Jetting Process

When you contact our team for help with hydro jetting pipes, you can expect a meticulous process done with the utmost care and efficiency. Here’s how it works:

1
We perform a video camera inspection to find and identify the clog.
2
We confirm that your pipes are strong enough to withstand the high pressure of the hydro jetting.
3

4

5
We reinsert the sewer line camera to confirm that the blockage is clear and the pipe walls are clean.
6
Once the clog is resolved, we close up the access point, clean up the work area, and provide you with maintenance information to prevent future clogs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is hydro jetting different from drain snaking?
Drain snaking punches a small hole through a clog, which restores some flow but usually allows the clog to reform before long. Hydro jetting completely scours the pipe clean, which offers a more long-term solution.
Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?
Hydro jetting can damage older, brittle pipes. We always confirm the condition and material of your pipes before attempting hydro jetting.
