Hydro Jetting vs Snaking: Which Do You Need

Cable snaking and hydro jetting both clear clogs, but they do very different jobs. Knowing which one your line needs saves money and keeps a backup from coming right back.

In West Jordan homes, a single soft clog often clears with a snake. Heavy grease, thick scale, or roots that keep returning usually call for hydro jetting, which cleans the full pipe wall instead of punching through the blockage.

Snaking is enough when:
  • You have one isolated, soft clog.
  • The blockage is close to the fixture.
  • The line is otherwise clean and clear.
  • You need a quick clear on a single drain.
Hydro jetting is the better choice when:
  • The line is coated with grease or hard water scale.
  • The same drain or main clogs again and again.
  • Roots have intruded into the sewer line.
  • Multiple fixtures back up from a shared line.
  • You want the pipe cleaned back to full diameter.

Is hydro jetting safe for my pipes?
For pipe in sound condition, hydro jetting is safe and highly effective. That’s why we camera the line first on older West Jordan homes: if we find cracked or badly corroded pipe, we’ll recommend the right approach instead of jetting a line that needs repair.
