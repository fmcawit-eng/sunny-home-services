When Do You Need Emergency Plumbing Services?

If you return from a great day at Wheels Park and notice a plumbing issue, how can you tell if you require emergency plumbing services? Look for these signs that may indicate it’s time to call our experienced plumbers for emergency repairs:

  • ✓ Major leaks: If water is gushing from your pipes or appliances, contact Sunny Home Services for emergency plumbing service right away.
  • ✓ Sewage backups: Any signs of gray water or sewage waste overflowing into toilets, floor drains, or tubs should be addressed immediately to prevent health hazards.
  • ✓ Overflowing toilets: If your toilet overflows and floods your bathroom and it won’t stop running after adjusting the flapper or valves, contact Sunny Home Services for same-day repairs.
  • ✓ Gas leaks: If you notice the odor of natural gas near your water line or water heater, you may have a leak that constitutes an urgent emergency.
  • ✓ Sump pump problems: Heavy rain may cause your basement to fill with water if the sump pump malfunctions, leading to flooding and water damage.
  • ✓ No water: If you have absolutely no water coming from your fixtures, schedule emergency service with Sunny Home Services as soon as possible.
  • ✓ Water heater issues: A water heater that bursts or starts leaking water is a sign you need emergency plumbing services.

What to Do Before Our 24 Hour Plumbers Arrive

Here’s what to do and how to prepare before an experienced 24-hour plumber from Sunny Home Services arrives at your West Jordan home:

1
Turn your water off at the local valve or at the main shut-off valve.
2
Switch the power or gas to your water heater off (when applicable).
3
Turn circuit breakers designated for leaking outlets, appliances, or water heaters off at the main breaker panel.
4
If you have issues with a gas water heater or gas appliance, turn off the main gas supply or set the pilot light to “off.”
5
Remove as much standing water as possible using towels and a mop and bucket to help dry out flooded areas.
6
Protect your belongings by moving furniture, area rugs, electronics, and other valuables out of wet areas in the home.

Why West Jordan Homes Are Prone to Plumbing Emergencies

You may wonder why so many homes in West Jordan are prone to plumbing emergencies. These emergencies may occur due to:

  • ✓ Hard water: West Jordan’s water supply has a very high content of minerals like calcium and magnesium with a hardness scale of approximately 10 to 25+ grains per gallon that creates intense scale buildup in valves, pipes, and water heater tanks, leading to leaks and water damage.
  • ✓ Freezing weather: Extremely cold, sub-zero winter snaps in our region can freeze uninsulated exterior pipes, causing cracks and bursting when the pipes thaw.
  • ✓ Soil conditions: Heavy clay soil in the Wasatch Front expands during periods of heavy rain and contracts when it dries, putting excess stress on sewer lines and underground pipes.
  • ✓ Aging infrastructure: Older homes built in the 1970s and 1980s may still have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes that are vulnerable to leaks and corrosion.
  • ✓ Tree root intrusion: As trees mature, their roots grow down into the ground, infiltrating clay or cast iron pipes, leading to massive backups that require professional sewer line inspection and repair.
