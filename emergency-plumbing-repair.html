When You Need Emergency Plumbing vs a Scheduled Repair

Not every plumbing problem is an emergency, but some can’t wait until morning. Knowing the difference helps you protect your home and avoid paying for urgent service you don’t need.

On the Wasatch Front homes, active water damage, no water at all, or a sewage backup are true emergencies. A slow drip or a single slow drain can usually be booked for the next available visit without risking your home.

Call for emergency service when:
  • A pipe has burst or water is actively spreading.
  • Your water heater is leaking or has ruptured.
  • Sewage is backing up into tubs or floor drains.
  • You’ve shut off the water and still see leaking.
  • You have no water to the home at all.
A scheduled repair is fine when:
  • A faucet drips slowly but the shutoff works.
  • One drain is slow but not fully blocked.
  • A running toilet wastes water without overflowing.
  • You want a fixture replaced or upgraded.
  • The issue is contained and not causing damage.
