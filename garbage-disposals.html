Common Garbage Disposal Problems That Require a Repair

Most garbage disposal repairs in West Jordan start with a unit that hums, leaks, or backs up, and the cause is usually hard water or grease. Here are common signs that your disposal should be looked at:

  • ✓Humming with no spinning
  • ✓Water pooling under your sink
  • ✓Slow draining or repeated backups
  • ✓Rattling from a loose mount or stuck object
  • ✓A reset button that won’t stay set

West Jordan’s water is hard, drawn mostly from the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District. Our water’s mineral load builds up on the grinding chamber and drain fittings, so disposals here corrode faster than they would in areas with softer water.

Grease is the other big culprit for garbage disposal problems. Fats and oils go down your sink warm, then cool and harden on the blades of a disposal unit, jam the motor, or clog the plumbing line behind it. Catching problems early means we can usually do a same-day repair and have your sink working again in one visit.

Repair vs. Replace Garbage Disposal

The decision to repair or replace a garbage disposal comes down to how old your unit is and the problem it’s having. We’ll likely steer you toward a replacement when:

  • ✓A leak is coming from the unit’s body, not a loose fitting.
  • ✓Your disposal is past the eight- to 10-year mark.
  • ✓The disposal jams repeatedly.
  • ✓The motor won’t reset, no matter how many times you try.

For minor issues in a newer unit, it’s usually worth calling a plumber to fix a garbage disposal rather than buying a new one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my garbage disposal humming but not spinning?
A disposal that won’t spin is likely jammed by hardened debris or an object that’s stuck against the grinding plate. A plumber can clear it and check for hidden damage before you try running it again.
How long do garbage disposals typically last?
Most disposals last eight to 12 years, though West Jordan’s hard water can corrode parts faster and shorten that timeline. If your unit is older and jamming often, it usually costs less in the long run to replace it.
Why does your disposal smell bad even after cleaning it?
Trapped food, grease buildup, and mildew can cause a lingering smell. Ice and citrus peels clear surface residue, but a smell that returns can mean standing water in the trap.
Is it worth repairing an old disposal or better to replace it?
Repairing an old disposal makes sense for a jam or a minor fix on a newer unit. Replacing an older, leaking disposal is usually the smarter move once it passes the eight- to 10-year mark, since repeat repairs add up fast.
Can you use your sink if the garbage disposal is broken?
Yes, if your garbage disposal is broken, you can still use your sink for rinsing, but don’t run food scraps through it. Turn off the switch until a plumber repairs or replaces it.
