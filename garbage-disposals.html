Common Garbage Disposal Issues We Handle

Disposal problems on the Wasatch Front usually come down to a handful of recurring issues driven by hard water, unit age, and what gets sent down the drain.

Utah’s mineral-heavy water corrodes internal parts, while grease and fibrous food overwhelm smaller units. Add in a leaky seal or a jammed flywheel, and a simple kitchen chore turns into a mess fast.

Sunny Home Services plumbers are frequently called out to address:

  • Disposals that hum but will not turn, usually a jam.
  • Units that are completely dead and need a reset or replacement.
  • Leaks from the sink flange, drain connection, or unit body.
  • Persistent backups and slow draining into the sink.
  • Loud rattling from loose parts or foreign objects.
  • Corroded grinding chambers worn down by hard water.

On the Wasatch Front specifically, we see disposal problems tied to both newer and older homes:

  • In newer homes, builder-grade units often burn out early under heavy family use.
  • In older homes across the valley, aging units and corroded drain fittings leak and clog more often.

Because a leaking or jammed disposal ties up the whole sink, we carry common units and parts on the truck so most repairs and swaps are handled in a single visit.

Signs You Need Garbage Disposal Repair vs Replacement

Knowing whether to repair a garbage disposal on the Wasatch Front, or replace it outright, comes down to the age of the unit, the type of problem, and how hard Utah water has worn it down.

On the Wasatch Front homes, a jam or a loose connection is often a quick repair, while a unit that leaks from the body or keeps tripping its reset is usually near the end of its life. When a disposal is old and corroded, replacing it is typically cheaper than repeated service calls.

Repair is the right choice when:
  • The unit is jammed but otherwise runs when cleared.
  • A leak is coming from a fitting or connection, not the body.
  • The reset button restores normal operation.
  • The disposal is only a few years old and works well when clear.
Replacement becomes more likely when:
  • Water is leaking from the body or shell of the unit.
  • The motor is dead and will not reset.
  • The unit is more than 8 to 10 years old.
  • It jams or clogs repeatedly no matter what goes in.
  • Hard water has corroded the grinding chamber.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my garbage disposal hum but not spin?
A humming disposal that will not spin is almost always jammed by a stuck object or hardened debris. The motor is getting power but the grinding plate cannot turn. Sunny Home Services can free the jam, check the unit for damage, and confirm it runs safely before we leave.
How long does a garbage disposal last on the Wasatch Front?
Most disposals last around 8 to 12 years, though Utah’s hard water can shorten that by corroding internal parts. If your unit is at that age and leaking or jamming often, replacement is usually more cost-effective than continued repairs.
Can you replace my disposal with a stronger unit?
Yes. We can upgrade a builder-grade unit to a more powerful, quieter model that better fits a busy kitchen and stands up to hard water. We help you choose the right size and install it the same visit whenever possible.
