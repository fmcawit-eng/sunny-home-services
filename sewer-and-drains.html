When to Clean, Repair, or Replace a Sewer Line

Knowing whether your West Jordan line needs a simple cleaning, a targeted repair, or a full replacement comes down to the condition of the pipe, how often it backs up, and what a camera inspection actually shows underground.

A one-time clog is usually a cleaning job. Recurring backups, roots, or cracked pipe point toward repair or replacement. If your line is old clay or cast iron and failing in multiple spots, replacement is often the more cost-effective path than repeated emergency clearings.

Cleaning or repair is often enough when:
  • The backup is isolated to one drain or fixture.
  • A camera shows grease, scale, or a single root intrusion.
  • The pipe itself is structurally sound.
  • Damage is limited to one section of the line.
Replacement becomes more likely when:
  • Backups keep returning even after cleaning.
  • The camera reveals collapsed, offset, or heavily cracked pipe.
  • Roots have invaded multiple joints along the line.
  • The sewer is aging clay or cast iron near the end of its life.
  • The line has bellied or sagged from ground shifting.
