Drain cleaning & clog removal

Recurring or slow drains

What Is Considered a Sewer and Drain Emergency?

A sewer and drain emergency can involve a backup, gurgling toilet, or other signs that wastewater is not draining properly. Multiple drains backing up at once, sewage odors, or water pooling near a sewer line can indicate a more serious plumbing problem that warrants prompt professional attention.

Signs You Have a Sewer Line Problem

You may have a problem farther down the drainage system if you notice:

  • Multiple slow drains throughout the house.
  • Repeated backups.
  • Gurgling toilets.
  • Sewage odors.

A sewer camera inspection can help with leak detection to pinpoint the cause of a sewer-line problem. West Jordan City places responsibility for the sewer lateral from the building out to and including the connection to the city sewer main on the property owner. The city is responsible for public sewer mains and manholes. If wastewater is backing up, contacting the city can help determine whether the blockage sits in the private lateral or the public system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I have a clogged drain or a sewer line problem?
A single slow drain is associated with a localized clog, while multiple drains backing up at the same time can point to a problem farther down the drainage system. Gurgling sounds and sewage odors are additional warning signs.
How much does sewer line repair cost in West Jordan?
Cost depends on the length of pipe affected, the repair method, and whether trenchless options are possible. A camera inspection can help determine the scope of the problem.
What causes recurring drain clogs?
Recurring clogs can come from grease buildup, hair, or a partial blockage further down the pipe. Bellied pipes and minor root intrusion are common underlying causes as well.
Can tree roots really damage a sewer line?
Tree roots can enter sewer pipes through cracks or separated joints. Older clay sewer infrastructure can be particularly vulnerable when joints or sections of pipe have deteriorated.
Do you use camera inspection before recommending repairs?
A camera inspection can help identify the location and cause of a sewer-line problem before repair or replacement. Whether an inspection is appropriate depends on the symptoms and the condition of the line.
Will I need to dig up my yard to repair a sewer line?
Depending on the location and condition of the pipe, trenchless methods may reduce excavation requirements. A professional inspection can determine whether a trenchless approach is appropriate.
How often should sewer lines be inspected in older homes?
Older properties, recurring backups, root intrusion, or other warning signs may justify a sewer camera inspection. Our sewer plumbers in West Jordan, UT, can recommend an inspection schedule based on the property’s age, pipe condition, and service history.
What should I do if my drains are backing up right now?
Stop running water in the home and avoid flushing toilets until the cause of the backup is identified. Contact a plumbing professional promptly, especially if wastewater is entering the home or multiple drains are affected.
