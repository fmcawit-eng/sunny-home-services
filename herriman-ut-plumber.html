Tankless water heaters, common in some newer Herriman construction, require different maintenance than standard tank models. Periodic descaling can help manage mineral buildup and maintain efficient operation, particularly where water hardness is higher.

What Counts as a Plumbing Emergency

Not every plumbing issue needs a call at 2 a.m., but some genuinely can’t wait. Here’s what we consider a true emergency:

  • ✓ Burst or actively leaking pipes
  • ✓ Sewage backups
  • ✓ Major water leaks that threaten flooring, drywall, or foundations
  • ✓ Plumbing problems that leave a home without essential water service

If you smell gas or suspect a gas line leak, leave the house and call the appropriate utility or emergency service first.

What to Do Before Our Plumbers Arrive

Shutting off your main water valve is the single most useful step you can take while help is on the way. After that, move valuables away from standing water and keep clear of outlets, appliances, or equipment that may have gotten wet.

Herriman’s Water & Housing Considerations for Plumbing Service Needs

Herriman sits along the base of the Oquirrh Mountains, and its layout shapes what our certified plumbers encounter. Herriman’s residential growth accelerated dramatically after the 1990s, so much of the city’s housing stock is relatively new compared with communities with longer-established residential development. Newer plumbing systems can still develop leaks, connection problems, drainage issues, or other maintenance needs as homes age.

Elevation and lot grading vary across Herriman, from lower-lying areas near the eastern portions of the city to foothill and hillside development farther west. That variation can affect water pressure and drainage, so a pressure issue in one neighborhood shouldn’t be assumed to have the same cause somewhere else in the city.
