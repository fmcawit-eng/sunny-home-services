Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a plumbing service call cost in Salt Lake City?
Plumbing service call costs vary depending on the type of service needed. For example, a full piping replacement will be more expensive than a faucet leak fix.
Why does my water heater run out of hot water so fast?
If your water heater is running out of water quickly, it is either due to an increase in your hot water consumption or an inefficient system. Look into upgrading to a tankless model.
How do I know if I have a slab leak?
If you suspect a slab leak, turn off all your water sources and listen for the sound of running water. You can also check your home water meter and see if there is movement even with the water off.
