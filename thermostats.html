Is It Time to Upgrade Your Thermostat?

Deciding whether to upgrade to a smart thermostat along the Wasatch Front comes down to what your current thermostat can and can’t do, and how much control you want over energy use.

Along the Wasatch Front homes, where systems run long in both summer and winter, an outdated thermostat can quietly cost you money all year. If several of the signals below apply, an upgrade usually pays for itself over time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a smart thermostat really save money along the Wasatch Front?
For most Wasatch Front homes, yes. Because systems run long hours in both the 100°F summers and freezing winters, smart scheduling and setbacks cut runtime when you don’t need it. The savings depend on your home and habits, but a properly configured thermostat typically pays for itself over time.
Is my HVAC system compatible with a smart thermostat?
Most systems are, but compatibility depends on your wiring and equipment. Many Utah homes need a C-wire for steady power, and heat pumps or multi-stage systems require specific configuration. Our technicians confirm compatibility before installing and handle any wiring the thermostat needs.
How long does a thermostat installation take?
Most smart thermostat installations are completed in about an hour, including wiring, testing, Wi-Fi setup, and configuring the schedule. More complex systems or wiring updates may take a little longer, but our technicians finish in a single visit whenever possible.
