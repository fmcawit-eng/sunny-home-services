Sewer Line Replacement & Installation in West Jordan, UT

Book Now Call 385.853.8116

At Sunny Home Services, we provide sewer line installation and replacement in West Jordan and across the Salt Lake Valley. With the valley’s shifting clay soil pulling joints apart in aging lines, mature tree roots working their way into cracked pipes, and hard water leaving a scale that narrows pipes from the inside, sewer systems here don’t always make it to their expected lifespan.

When your line starts backing up, drains slow down throughout your house, or sewage smells start lingering in your basement, our team can be there fast to identify the problem and recommend the right replacement approach. We offer minimally invasive options and trenchless solutions that get your system flowing again.

When Do You Need Sewer Line Replacement in Your West Jordan Home

You need sewer line replacement when your pipes are nearing the end of their service life, have severe damage, or completely collapse. A camera inspection is the quickest way to confirm it, but you’ll also notice a few warning signs before they fail:

  • ✓ Backups that return within weeks of being cleared.
  • ✓ Multiple slow drains throughout your house.
  • ✓ Sewage smells in your basement or crawl space.
  • ✓ Soggy or bright green patches in your lawn.
  • ✓ A line that needs drain cleaning more than twice a year.

Our Sewer Line Replacement Options

We provide traditional and trenchless sewer line replacement methods, depending on your pipe’s condition and location. Our methods include:

  • Traditional excavation: For lines with severe damage and grade problems, we open a trench along the line, remove the old pipe, and lay new PVC.
  • Pipe bursting: Our technicians pull a new pipe through the path of the old one, fracturing the existing pipe outward as it goes, which requires only two small access points.
  • Pipe lining (CIPP): A resin-covered liner cures inside the existing pipe and forms a new pipe within it.

We Will Help Choose the Right Method for Your Property in the Wasatch Front

The right replacement method for your home depends on your pipe, soil, and what’s on top of the line. We perform a camera inspection first, review the footage with you, and make recommendations from there.

Trenchless is our preferred method, helping to preserve your property when the line runs under a driveway, mature tree, or landscaping. Excavation becomes the better alternative when the pipe has collapsed, the slope needs correcting, or the line runs through an open front yard.

When we review your options, we’ll lay out the pricing for each method so you can make the right decision for your budget. We’ll never pressure you into a more expensive option than your line needs or upsell you on services that aren’t required.

What to Consider When Replacing Sewer Lines in West Jordan, UT

When replacing sewer lines in West Jordan, you need to consider key factors like the cost, disruption to your yard, and timing. The area’s climate and soil add a few additional considerations, including:

  • ✓ Winter frost: Excavation becomes more challenging when the ground freezes in Utah, causing prices to increase after the first freeze.
  • ✓ Landscaping: Your soil type, sprinkler lines, tree roots, and hardscape all factor into the method choice and restoration requirements.
  • ✓ Timing: If you’re already replacing your sewer line, you can cut long-term costs by addressing old galvanized supply lines or a failing water heater during the same visit.
  • ✓ Financing: When your sewer lines fail unexpectedly, our team can set you up with a flexible financing plan and explore special offers to make your costs more manageable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does homeowners insurance cover sewer pipe replacement?
Sewer pipe replacement is covered by homeowners insurance if the damage results from a sudden, covered event. Most policies won’t cover gradual deterioration, root intrusion, or everyday wear.
How long does a new sewer line last?
Sewer lines last between 50 and 100 years, depending on the material, soil conditions, and maintenance schedule. If your sewer line is nearing the end of its lifespan, schedule a camera inspection with our team to determine if it’s ready for replacement.
How do I know if I need replacement instead of repair?
If your sewer line has collapsed, has separated joints, or holds standing water in a belly, it likely requires replacement instead of repair. A camera inspection can tell us what situation you’re in and help you make the right call.
