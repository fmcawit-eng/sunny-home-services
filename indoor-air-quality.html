Indoor Air Quality Solutions We Offer on the Wasatch Front and The Wasatch Front Area

After thoroughly checking the indoor air quality of your home, we offer several professional IAQ solutions. With Sunny Home Services, homeowners in the Wasatch Front area get reliable options that are designed to last.

Air Duct Cleaning

Our professional air duct cleaning service thoroughly removes pollen, dust mites, and pet dander from ductwork to keep your home free of a variety of common allergens and irritants. This service also helps to improve HVAC system efficiency by reducing strain and lowering your monthly energy bills. Routine air duct cleaning also keeps pests at bay for a safer home overall.

Ductwork Installation

If your current ductwork is loose, leaky, or damaged, we provide professional ductwork installation services to fix the issue. New ductwork that’s installed properly and free of gaps or holes ensures that the air circulating into your home is fresh and clean. Whole-Home Air Filtration Systems

A whole-home air filtration system integrates directly into your HVAC equipment to provide consistent, reliable filtration. This advanced IAQ solution removes a range of airborne particles and pollutants, including pollen, mold spores, and biological pathogens. Not only is it highly effective at maintaining good air quality, but it also offers quiet operation and easy maintenance.

Learn More ›
Whole-House Humidifiers

Low outdoor humidity levels on the Wasatch Front can make the air inside your home uncomfortably dry. Installing a whole-house humidifier remedies this issue by adding moisture to treated air directly at the source. Unlike a standalone humidifier, whole-home humidifiers work to treat your entire home at once, preventing issues like sore throat, dry eyes, itchy skin, and other symptoms associated with dry air.

Learn More ›
Whole-House Dehumidifiers

A whole-house dehumidifier removes excess moisture from the air to prevent mold and mildew spores from growing and spreading. This IAQ solution also halts the reproduction of dust mites to provide relief to homeowners who suffer from asthma flare-ups. Lowering your home’s relative humidity also keeps temperatures more comfortable while reducing wear and tear on your HVAC system.

Learn More ›
UV Germicidal Lights

Ultraviolet germicidal lights are installed in your HVAC air ducts or on the evaporator coils. These powerful lights neutralize airborne and surface pathogens by disrupting the DNA of mold spores, bacteria, and viruses. UV lights work very well for this purpose, but they don’t remove dust or odors.

Common Air Quality Issues Our Technicians See on the Wasatch Front Homes

If you live in Utah’s Wasatch Front area, you may encounter a range of indoor air quality issues. Here’s what our technicians typically see on the Wasatch Front homes:

  • ✓ Low humidity: Due to the arid climate and our geographic makeup, many homeowners have extremely low indoor humidity levels, prompting the need for a whole-home humidifier to keep the air moist and comfortable.
  • ✓ High pollen: Birch, juniper, maple, cottonwood, and oak are the biggest contributors of tree pollen during February through May, while grass pollen levels peak in the summer and weed pollen peaks from August through October.
  • ✓ Particulate matter: These fine particles are worse on the Wasatch Front during the winter and can exacerbate symptoms in those with cardiovascular and respiratory issues.
  • ✓ Ozone: When nitrogen oxides and Volatile Organic Compounds (VOCs) react on hot, sunny days, ozone levels rise.
  • ✓ Wildfire smoke and dust: Dust blowing from the Great Salt Lake increases dust on interior surfaces, while drifting smoke from wildfires can easily infiltrate Wasatch Front homes.

What’s Included in Our Home IAQ Testing & Assessment

To select the most effective IAQ solution for your household, we recommend scheduling a professional home IAQ testing and assessment with Sunny Home Services, which includes:

1
Walkthrough
A certified tech checks your home for visible issues like dust buildup, mold, and odors, and performs a thorough moisture and leak check to look for any hidden water damage or mildew growth.
2
HVAC Inspection
We carefully inspect your HVAC system, ductwork, and air filter for poor airflow and potential contaminants.
3
Particulate Matter Test
Our team measures the density of particulate matter in the air, like pollen, mold spores, and airborne dust.
4
VOC Evaluation
We thoroughly evaluate VOC levels in your home. These can accumulate through off-gassing from carpet, paints, household cleaning products, and building materials.
5
Carbon Monoxide Test
Our techs track the level of fresh air ventilation in your home and determine whether there are any risks associated with possible carbon monoxide combustion.
6
Relative Humidity
We test your home’s relative indoor humidity level to determine whether a dehumidifier or humidifier is needed.
7
Sampling
If any issues are found during the initial assessment, we collect samples of various allergens, mold, or radon and send them to a laboratory for testing.
8
Report
Once the testing and assessment are complete, we give you a detailed written report that includes any lab findings (when applicable) and provide professional recommendations to improve the indoor air quality in your home.
