Signs your AC needs repair

Here are signs to watch for that tell you your AC needs repair:

  • Airflow is weak, or the air feels warm and isn’t cooling the house.
  • The system keeps turning on and off without cooling.
  • Ice is building up on the components.
  • The system is making unusual noises.
  • Your energy bills have suddenly increased without a change in usage.

Common AC repairs we perform

The AC repairs we handle most often include:

  • Electrical problems that stop the system from starting or running properly.
  • Fan or motor problems that affect airflow.
  • Refrigerant problems that cause warm air or poor cooling.
  • Frozen coils that stop the system from cooling adequately.
  • Drainage problems that cause water to pool around the unit.
  • Thermostat issues that keep your AC from reaching the set temperature.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I replace my furnace and air conditioner at the same time?
Your furnace and AC share the same blower and ductwork, so replacing them together means matched equipment, airflow set up once, and one installation instead of two. If your furnace is significantly newer, we’ll tell you that and quote the AC on its own.
My house is only 15 years old. Why does the AC already need replacing?
Your AC needs replacing because equipment ages on its own schedule, not the home’s. Many Lehi houses went up in the 2000s boom with builder-grade systems installed at minimum efficiency, and 12 to 15 years is a normal service life.
Is my air conditioner still under warranty?
Your air conditioner may still be under warranty, and it’s worth checking before approving a major repair. Manufacturer parts warranties often run five to ten years from installation. Most require proof of annual maintenance.
