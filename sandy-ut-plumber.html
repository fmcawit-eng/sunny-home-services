What Counts as a Plumbing Emergency

A true plumbing emergency is anything that poses a risk of water damage, contamination, or a total loss of water service. That can include:

  • ✓ Burst pipes
  • ✓ Sewage backups
  • ✓ Major water leaks
  • ✓ Other problems that threaten significant water damage or leave a home without essential water service

If you suspect a gas line leak, leave the area and contact the appropriate emergency utility or emergency service.

What to Do Before Our Plumbers Arrive

Shutting off your main water valve is the single most useful thing you can do before help shows up. After that, move valuables away from standing water and stay away from electrical outlets, appliances, or equipment that may have been exposed to water.

Sandy’s Water & Housing Considerations for Plumbing Service Needs

Sandy sits at the base of the Wasatch Mountains, and that geography shapes what our plumbers see day to day. Sandy’s municipal water is moderately hard, particularly with calcium carbonate, and that mineral content can contribute to scale on fixtures and inside water-using appliances.

Housing stock varies by neighborhood. Plumbing materials and system age can vary considerably from one property to another. Newer homes near the Sandy-Draper border can have different plumbing layouts and equipment from older properties.
