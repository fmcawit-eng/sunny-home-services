Signs your furnace needs repair

Keep an eye out for these warning signs that your furnace needs attention:

  • Cool air is blowing from the vents
  • Your furnace starts and shuts off repeatedly
  • The temperature is different in every room
  • You hear rattling, scraping, or squealing noises
  • Your utility costs are rising

Common furnace repairs we perform

Our technicians complete most furnace repairs in a single trip, commonly fixing:

  • Burned-out ignitors or coated flame sensors
  • Stuck or clogged pressure switches
  • Gas valve or burner problems
  • Blocked or frozen condensate lines
  • Thermostat or electrical malfunctions

Heating Maintenance & Tune-Ups in Draper, UT

What’s Included in a Furnace Tune-Up

Every tune-up covers:

  • Heat exchanger inspection
  • Burner and flame sensor cleaning
  • Safety switch and limit control testing
  • Gas pressure and combustion verification
  • Filter inspection and replacement
  • Condensate drain flush
  • Whole-home humidifier pad and water line check

Why Fall Maintenance Matters Before Winter Hits

Scheduling maintenance in early fall gives us time to find hidden problems before your furnace has to run around the clock. Weak igniters, dirty sensors, and worn capacitors almost always fail during the first cold snap of the season, when service schedules are already full.
