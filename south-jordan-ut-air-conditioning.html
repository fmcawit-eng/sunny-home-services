Air Conditioner Repair in South Jordan, UT

Signs your AC needs repair

As an attentive homeowner, keep an eye out for these signs that you need air conditioner repairs near you:

  • Short cycling.
  • The AC is making strange noises (hissing, banging, and grinding).
  • The AC is emanating strange smells (musty or burning).
  • The AC is dripping water.

Ductless mini-split installation

For older South Jordan, UT homes without existing ductwork, ductless mini-split installations are available. These systems allow for independent zoning controls, perfect for room additions, sunrooms, offices, garages, and basement suites.

Swamp cooler to central AC conversion

Evaporative air or “swamp coolers” are good for desert air conditions like those in South Jordan, but they require a tremendous amount of upkeep. You need to change the water reservoir and maintain the pads to prevent mold. Sunny Home Services will help you convert from a swamp cooler to a central AC system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does air conditioner repair cost in South Jordan?
Air conditioner repair costs vary based on the type of repair needed. Minor refrigerant leak sealing is on the lower end, while replacing a fan belt is on the higher end.
How long does AC installation take?
Most AC installations take four to eight hours, depending on your home setup and any needed upgrades. During the estimation, our trained technicians will give you a time estimate.
What size AC unit do I need for my home?
Only a trained AC technician can determine the exact sizing of the unit needed. However, a good starting point is 20 BTUs per square foot.
Will a new AC system lower my energy bills?
Yes, a new AC system with a SEER rating will almost always lower your home energy bills. A new system will also work more efficiently than an aging system.
