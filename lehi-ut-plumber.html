Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a plumbing service call cost in Lehi?
The cost of plumbing service calls in Lehi varies based on several factors, such as the job’s complexity and the time required to complete it. If you need emergency plumbing services after hours, your cost may be higher.
How do I know if I have a slab leak?
If your water bill is suddenly much higher than normal or you experience low water pressure, you may have a slab leak. Other signs include wet spots on your floors, warm spots, cracks in walls or floors, or hearing water running when all the taps are off.
How often should I have my plumbing inspected?
We recommend scheduling a professional plumbing inspection once per year to keep your system running safely. If you live in an older home or if you have a large household, you may want to schedule plumbing maintenance twice per year.
