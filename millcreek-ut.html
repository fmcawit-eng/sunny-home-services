Common HVAC Problems We See in Millcreek Homes

The Salt Lake Valley has hot summers and cold, snowy winters, so a reliable HVAC system is essential. Our team has experience resolving a wide range of HVAC issues. Some of the most common problems we see include:

  • ✓Poor air circulation: Dirty filters, frozen AC coils, mechanical failures, and ductwork problems can hinder airflow, forcing your HVAC to work harder to keep you comfortable.
  • ✓No airflow: If the vents are open and the system is on, no airflow may mean a broken fan or a tripped breaker.
  • ✓Uneven heating and cooling: Hot and cold spots could mean a malfunctioning thermostat, dirty air filters, or poor insulation.
  • ✓Short cycling: If the system turns on and off quickly, it could be due to a clogged air filter, low refrigerant, or thermostat problems.
  • ✓Strange noises: If you hear grinding, squealing, hissing, or other sounds coming from your HVAC system, it could mean broken parts, leaks, or other system stressors.
  • ✓Lack of maintenance: We offer seasonal HVAC maintenance, recommended twice a year in spring and fall, to keep your system running smoothly.

Signs Your System Needs To Be Replaced

Consider HVAC replacement in your Millcreek home if:

  • ✓Your heater and AC are reaching the end of their lifespans: Most HVAC systems are designed to work efficiently for 10 to 15 years and will begin to show wear as they age.
  • ✓The repair costs are high: In some cases, replacing your furnace and AC is more cost-effective than continuing to repair a failing system.
  • ✓The air conditioner uses outdated R-22 refrigerant: Certain refrigerants are no longer used, so replacement parts may be hard to find, and recharging is expensive.
  • ✓You have an improperly sized HVAC system: If your system is too big or too small, you may want to replace it for more efficient operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for new HVAC installation in my home?
Yes, you need a city permit when replacing or installing HVAC equipment. You also need a permit to add, remove, or alter ductwork and plumbing systems in your home.
Will a new HVAC system lower my energy bills?
Yes, if it’s installed by a professional, a new HVAC system will run more efficiently than your old system, which can lower your energy bills by up to 50%. This percentage may be higher if your ductwork and home insulation are in good shape.
What size HVAC unit do I need for my home?
Our HVAC technicians perform a Manual J® Load calculation to determine the optimal size of heater and AC for your home. They consider multiple factors, including square footage, windows and sun exposure, ceiling height, insulation quality, and Utah’s local climate.
