Furnace Repair Services in Lehi, UT

Our furnace repair services cover gas, electric, and heat pump furnaces across every neighborhood in Lehi.

Signs your furnace needs repair

Some of the top warning signs your furnace needs repair include:

  • Cold or lukewarm air coming from the vents.
  • Your furnace short-cycling every few minutes.
  • Uneven temperatures from room to room.
  • Banging, grinding, or squealing noises.
  • Burning or metallic smells during operation.

Common furnace repairs we perform

Our technicians commonly solve the following issues, often in a single visit:

  • Failing igniters and flame sensors
  • Dirty or failing pressure switches
  • Worn blower motors and capacitors
  • Gas valve problems
  • Cracked or clogged condensate lines
  • Faulty thermostat wiring

Lehi’s Climate Considerations for Heating Service

Living in Lehi means freezing winter nights, high-elevation air, and strong canyon winds that put added strain on your heating equipment. At 4,500 feet or higher, homes here have to deal with thin air and overnight lows that keep the furnace running all season long.

Winter inversions can also trap cold air and particulate in the valley for days, causing your furnace to work overtime while your indoor air quality plummets. With canyon winds blowing in and wide temperature swings from day to night, your system stays cycling more than it would in other climates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my furnace blowing cold air?
When your furnace starts blowing cold air, it’s often due to a dirty flame sensor, incorrect fan setting, or clogged filter. Our technicians can perform diagnostics to determine the root cause and explain your repair options.
How do I know if my furnace is a carbon monoxide risk?
If your furnace burns with a yellow flame, leaves soot around the burner, or causes headaches or nausea that go away when you leave the area, you may have a carbon monoxide leak. Immediately leave your home, contact emergency services, and call us once your home has been cleared.
Do I need a permit for a new furnace installation?
Most furnace installations in Lehi require a permit from Lehi City’s Building Department. We handle the process so you don’t have to worry about coordinating with the city.
