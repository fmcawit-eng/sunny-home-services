Benefits of Tankless Water Heaters Compared to Traditional Tank Heaters

Tankless water heaters, also called on-demand water heaters, heat water as required to serve your home. Because they don’t continuously reheat water in a tank, they’re significantly more energy efficient than traditional water heaters. Just a few of the benefits of tankless water heaters include:

  • ✓ Significant energy savings: Tankless water heaters only heat water when it’s required, so there’s no extra energy used to constantly keep water hot.
  • ✓ Lower utility bills: Major energy savings translate to lower costs on your utility bills.
  • ✓ Continuous hot water: Tankless water heaters heat water on demand, providing continuous flow that never runs out.
  • ✓ Smaller footprint: Tankless systems mount directly to your wall, saving you valuable floor space.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you convert my existing tank water heater to tankless?
Yes, we can replace a water tank heater with a tankless system, although the process is typically more complex than simply swapping out the water heater unit. Depending on your current system, we may need to upgrade fuel lines, change the pipes, and make other accommodations to support the tankless system.
What size tankless unit do I need for my household?
Choosing the right size tankless water heater for your household depends on the number of people in your home as well as the number of water-using appliances that you plan to use at the same time. Our licensed plumbers can talk with you about your home water needs and match you with the perfect tankless system.
Does skipping maintenance void my tankless warranty?
Yes, in most cases, skipping professional maintenance will void your tankless water heater warranty. Most warranties require annual maintenance to maintain compliance, which is important for covering replacement part costs in the event of a breakdown.
How often does a tankless water heater need to be descaled?
It’s recommended to descale a tankless water heater at least once a year in order to maintain optimal efficiency. However, you may want to descale more frequently if you rely on well water, as the West Jordan area has relatively hard water with a high mineral content.
