Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for a new furnace installation?
Yes, permits through the Midvale City Building & Safety department are required for new furnace installations. These mechanical permits ensure that the venting, electrical hookups, and gas lines all comply with current building codes.
How do I know if my furnace is a carbon monoxide risk?
If you detect the odor of rotten eggs or if your pilot light is flickering and yellow or orange instead of blue, your furnace is a carbon monoxide risk. Furnaces with cracked or damaged heat exchangers are also at high risk of a dangerous carbon monoxide leak.
Will a new furnace lower my energy bills?
Installing a new furnace with a high AFUE rating can help to lower your energy bills. Modern furnaces have features like variable-speed motors, sealed combustion, and advanced filtration that help to lower energy costs and reduce waste.
