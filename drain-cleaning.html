Signs You Need Professional Drain Cleaning

Some slow drains clear with a plunger, but recurring or multiple clogs are a sign the line needs professional cleaning, or that something deeper is wrong. Knowing the difference saves you money and prevents a bigger backup.

On the Wasatch Front homes, a single slow drain usually points to local buildup. When several fixtures back up at once, or clogs keep returning, the problem is often further down the line and worth a closer look with a camera.

A single drain cleaning handles:
  • One slow or clogged sink, tub, or shower.
  • Buildup from grease, hair, soap, or scale.
  • A drain that responds to cleaning and stays clear.
  • Routine maintenance to prevent future clogs.
A deeper look is smart when:
  • Multiple fixtures back up at the same time.
  • The same drain clogs again within weeks.
  • You hear gurgling or smell sewage.
  • Water backs up into tubs or floor drains.
  • The home is older with clay or cast iron pipe.
