Drain Cleaning Services We Offer in West Jordan, UT

Our plumbers provide a comprehensive range of drain cleaning services in West Jordan.

Mechanical drain cleaning

Mechanical methods are one of the first strategies we use when providing plumbing drain cleaning services. This involves either a mechanical drain snake or auger, which allows us to physically punch through clogs of fat, oil, and grease, foreign objects, hair, soap scum, and more.

Hydro jetting

For stubborn clogs or lines that have been impeded by roots of water-hungry trees, mechanical drain cleaning methods may not be enough. In these cases, we use hydro jetting, which uses a stream of high-pressure water to cut through clogs or tree roots without damaging the surrounding pipe.

Drain inspections

To determine the exact cause of a drain clog, we’ll perform a camera inspection. Using a special waterproof camera, we can see what’s going on inside the drain or sewer line, which helps us find the best approach to resolve the issue.

Sewer line cleanouts

Our West Jordan clogged drain plumbers can help you quickly resolve sewer line backups, protecting you from unpleasant and hazardous sewage backups in your home. Cleanouts help sewage and wastewater reliably flow out of your home and into the main sewage system.

Leak detection

Over time, a severe drain or pipe clog can put enough pressure on your plumbing to cause leaks throughout the home. Because most plumbing is hidden from view, you’ll need professional leak detection to pinpoint and repair the issue without ripping up walls and floors.

Sewer line repairs

A damaged sewer line can cause sewage backups or allow hazardous waste to leak into your landscaping. We provide fast, reliable sewer line repair to help restore normal wastewater flow without the need for a new sewer installation. Whenever possible, we’ll perform trenchless sewer repair, which allows us to fix the problem without digging up your yard to access the line.

Emergency drain services

Plumbing emergencies come in all forms, and that can definitely include a clogged drain. We provide emergency plumbing and drain cleaning services in West Jordan, with fast response times that help you protect your home from major backups or whole-home drain failures.

Signs of a Clogged Drain in Your West Jordan Home

How do you know when it’s time to get in touch with a clogged drain service near you? Key signs to be on the lookout for as a homeowner include:

  • Slow water flow down your sink, shower, or tub drain.
  • Gurgling noises from pipes or toilets when emptying drains or flushing toilets.
  • Bad odors (e.g., sewage, rotten food, sulfuric smells) from drains.
  • Water or sewage backups in your drains.

If you notice any of these signs, the cause is likely a clogged drain. Depending on the symptoms you observe, the issue could indicate a more localized clog in just one drain, or a deeper clog in your main sewage line, requiring more extensive inspection and repair.

Clogged Drain Repair Options for Recurring Clogs

Some minor clogs can be temporarily cleared with DIY methods like chemical cleaners or a toilet plunger. However, these clogs return fairly quickly, as DIY methods only poke a small hole in the clog. If you want to truly resolve the issue, there’s no substitute for professional drain clearing. Working with a skilled plumber ensures complete removal of any buildup in your pipes, which not only makes a future clog far less likely but also stops any foul odors from wafting up from your drains.

What Causes Clogged Drains in West Jordan Homes

In our experience, the most common causes for clogged drains in the West Jordan area are:

  • ✓ Fat, oil, and grease: This is a common issue in kitchen sinks, with fat congealing and hardening into tough clogs.
  • ✓ Hair and soap scum: These are common clog-causers in bathroom sinks, showers, and tubs, especially because West Jordan’s hard water creates increased soap scum.
  • ✓ Non-flushable items: Wet wipes, paper towels, and hygiene products can expand and clog toilet drain pipes.
  • ✓ Mineral buildup: The very hard water in West Jordan can create a rough surface of mineral buildup, increasing the frequency of clogs in your drains.
  • ✓ Tree roots: The roots of tree species like cottonwoods, willows, poplars, and box elders can seek out drain pipes and break through, causing stubborn clogs.
  • ✓ Pipe damage: A sagging pipe or improper slope can prevent proper gravity draining.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does drain cleaning cost in West Jordan?
Drain cleaning costs in West Jordan vary by scope and the severity of the clog. You can expect the project to cost somewhere between $100 and $600, depending on the methods required.
Is it safe to use store-bought drain cleaner?
Store-bought drain cleaner is safe to use in newer pipes, but it tends to be rather ineffective. There’s also the risk of chemical burns or plumbing damage when used incorrectly, so we recommend avoiding it and calling a pro for a clogged drain.
How often should drains be professionally cleaned?
Sunny Home Services recommends preventative drain cleaning once a year. Regular drain cleaning prevents serious clogs and related plumbing problems.
