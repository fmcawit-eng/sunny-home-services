New Gas Line Installation in West Jordan and the Wasatch Front

Due to Utah natural gas being among the lowest prices in the United States, many homeowners opt for gas line hookups. Some common scenarios that require adding a gas line include:

  • ✓ Gas range/cooktop: These provide open flames for precise home cooking.
  • ✓ Outdoor fire pit: Gas fire pits allow you to provide outdoor flames without any smoke.
  • ✓ Home backup generator hookup: Home generators will power your home in the event of a power outage, saving the food in your fridge from going bad and providing power to your HVAC system and lights.
  • ✓ Gas dryer: Gas dryers offer faster drying times than electric units.
  • ✓ Water heater conversion: Some water heater installations, like tankless units, require gas power, which will usually mean connecting to an existing line or installing a new one.
  • ✓ Gas furnaces: Gas furnaces offer an immediate and convenient way to heat your home.

The total number of gas lines your home runs depends on the capacity of your main gas line. Sometimes, other lines need to be added and connected to the existing main line.

Our experienced natural gas line plumbers near you will look at your situation and determine if a new gas line installation is needed. In that instance, we will pull the appropriate gas line permits and properly size the new line, based on the appliances’ combined BTUs.

Gas Line Repair in West Jordan, UT

Sunny Home Services technicians are also well-versed in all types of gas line repairs, including:

  • Aging line corrosion: Older homes in West Jordan may still use cast-iron or galvanized steel for their gas line. These naturally corrode and need to be repaired or replaced.
  • Fitting issues: Loose connections, mismatched threadings, incorrect sealant usage, and corrosion all, over time, result in fitting issues that can be dangerous if not addressed quickly.
  • Appliance connector problems: The gas line connections directly on home appliances must be tightened properly to guarantee proper gas fuel flow.

Signs Your Gas Line Needs Repair

Some common signs indicate the need for gas line leak repair. As a diligent homeowner, be on the lookout for:

  • ✓ Hissing sounds: If you hear a faint hiss near the indoor or outdoor piping, this could signal a slow leak.
  • ✓ The smell of rotten eggs: The smell of rotten eggs almost always means a gas leak, which can pose health concerns.
  • ✓ Dead patches of grass: If you notice dead patches of grass in your yard, it can be due to gas seeping out of your underground line.
  • ✓ Weak appliance performance: If your gas-powered appliance is working inefficiently, it could be an issue with your gas line.
  • ✓ Visible rust or corrosion: Corrosion on your indoor piping usually means other sections of your underground pipe could be compromised.

What to Do if You Suspect a Gas Leak?

Gas leaks are dangerous emergencies and can bring health concerns like headaches, nausea, and more severe ailments if they are not addressed properly.

If you suspect your home has a gas leak, the best thing to do is leave the home immediately, dialing the gas company emergency line before calling a gas line repair plumber from Sunny Home Services. Avoid using any appliances, light switches, phones, etc., and only turn off your gas supply if the main shutoff valve is in a safe area away from the suspected leak. Never go looking for the leak yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit to install a new gas line?
Yes, the state of Utah requires permits for all new gas line installations. Local regions like West Jordan and the Wasatch Front have their own specific guidelines, which Sunny Home Services is aware of.
How much does gas line installation cost?
The cost of a single gas line installation in Utah averages from $500 to $1,500. This large cost variety is based on the length, type of materials, and the complexity of the gas line route needed.
How long does gas line repair take?
The length of time needed for gas line repairs varies on the type of repair needed. A simple appliance connection issue can usually be resolved in an hour, while a more involved repair, like a leak patch-up, can take multiple hours and sometimes days based on the condition of your pipe.
Is it safe to use my appliances if I suspect a leak?
No, it is not safe to use appliances if you suspect a gas leak. Even the smallest spark can trigger an explosion, so leave the premises and call Sunny Home Services.
