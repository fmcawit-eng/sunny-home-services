Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my furnace blowing cold air?
There are several reasons why your furnace may be blowing cold air, including a dirty pilot light sensor, clogged filters, or thermostat issues. One of our qualified technicians will perform a thorough inspection and get to the root of the issue.
How do I know if my furnace is a carbon monoxide risk?
If your pilot light flame is yellow or orange instead of blue, or if you notice large amounts of soot around your furnace, the system may be a carbon monoxide risk. The best way to test is to install a carbon monoxide detector for safety or consult a licensed HVAC technician.
Do I need a permit for a new furnace installation?
Yes, you need a permit for a new furnace installation in Taylorsville. When you work with Sunny Home Services, we’ll help you with the permitting process to make sure everything is ready to go before work starts.
