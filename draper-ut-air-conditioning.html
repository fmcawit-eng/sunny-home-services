Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my AC running but not cooling my home?
If your AC is running but not cooling your house, it could be due to a blocked air filter, a refrigerant leak, a dirty condenser, or an electrical issue. Contact our AC technicians for a professional diagnosis.
How long does AC installation take?
AC installation times depend on the type of system and its size. Most projects can be completed in under a day, though we’ll provide a timeline along with an upfront, no-surprise cost estimate.
Will a new AC system lower my energy bills?
Yes, a new AC system will likely reduce your energy bills. New, modern air conditioners run more efficiently and adhere to higher energy consumption standards.
