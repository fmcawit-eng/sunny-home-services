Replacement is not always necessary. In fact, toilet repair is the right solution when the fixture is structurally sound and it’s only a specific component that’s failed. Most toilet issues can be fixed quickly with the right parts and diagnosis.

Common Toilet Problems in West Jordan Homes

When you’re looking for a plumber for a leaking toilet, you’re likely dealing with one of a handful of common problems.

  • Running toilet: This is usually caused by a worn flapper or faulty fill valve.
  • Leaking at the base: This leaking is most often due to a failed wax ring or loose bolts.
  • Weak flush: Mineral buildup from the hard water, or internal component wear are usually to blame for weak or incomplete flushing.
  • Clogging: Most clogs are caused by excess paper, non-flushable items, buildup, or developing blockages in the line.
  • Tank not filling: When your tank doesn’t fill properly, it can usually be traced to a faulty fill valve or a supply issue.

For urgent situations like overflows or active leaks, emergency toilet repair is the fastest way to stop damage and restore function.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my toilet keep running?
Your toilet keeps running because internal components like the flapper or fill valve are no longer sealing properly, allowing water to continuously flow from the tank into the bowl. In many cases, this is a quick repair, but if the issue persists, replacement components or a full upgrade may be needed.
How do I know if I need a toilet repair or full replacement?
Whether you need a repair or replacement depends on the nature of the problem. If the issue is isolated, like a worn part or minor leak, you need a repair. If problems are recurring, the toilet is inefficient, or the fixture is damaged, you’re probably looking at a replacement.
Why is water leaking from the base of my toilet?
Water leaking from the base of your toilet is usually caused by a failed wax ring or loose mounting bolts. If left unaddressed, water from the base can lead to floor damage and mold, which is why it should be repaired as quickly as possible.
