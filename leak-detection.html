Signs You May Have a Hidden Leak in Your West Jordan Home

Signs that you have a hidden leak in your West Jordan home can develop gradually and subtly, but knowing what to watch for helps you to respond promptly and call a plumber for leak detection from Sunny Home Services. Contact our team for residential leak detection services if you notice these red flags:

  • ✓ High water bill: Even small leaks can cause a sudden jump in meter usage or water bills.
  • ✓ Musty odors or mold: Leaks create ideal mold conditions, encouraging it to grow and sometimes resulting in visible mold or musty odors around the home.
  • ✓ Damp spots: Water can travel far from the source of the leak and visibly saturate wall or ceiling materials, sometimes with brown or yellow water stain rings.
  • ✓ Bubbling or peeling paint: Leaks can saturate drywall and plaster and deteriorate paint, causing it to bubble, lift, and flake off.
  • ✓ Warped, lifted, or soft flooring: Hardwood, laminate, and subfloors absorb moisture, resulting in swelling, buckling, and eventual rotting.
  • ✓ Running water sounds: Hearing running water when the taps are off means that water is still moving through your pipes.
  • ✓ Low water pressure: Reduced volume from leaks drops the overall pressure in the pipes and at your faucets.

Leak Detection Methods We Use in the Wasatch Front

At Sunny Home Services, we use advanced, non-destructive plumbing leak detection methods in the Wasatch Front to find hidden leaks before they can escalate and cause further damage. We use technology and methods such as:

  • Acoustic listening devices: Identify sounds of pressurized or running water escaping through floors, concrete, soil, and other surfaces.
  • Thermal imaging cameras: Detect temperature variations in walls, ceilings, and floors from accumulating moisture to identify anomalies.
  • Video pipe inspections: Use a specialized plumbing camera on a flexible cable to inspect the inside of pipes and sewer drains for cracks, tree root intrusions, broken joints, and other leak sources.
  • Pressure testing: Isolates pipe segments and uses gauges to find water pressure drops.

With decades of experience and knowledge of typical plumbing and conditions in homes throughout the Wasatch Front, our team at Sunny Home Services can detect and repair a wide range of leaks. Common types of leaks we detect and repair in local homes include:

Factors That Affect Plumbing and Leak Risks in West Jordan Homes

Certain factors affect plumbing and leak risks in West Jordan homes, ranging from seasonal shifts to the municipal water supply. Sunny Home Services has the experience to identify and repair leaks due to local factors like:

  • ✓ Hard water: Mineral-rich water from the municipal supply builds up scaling and sediment in pipes and water heaters, which then increases water pressure, wears out rubber seals, and accelerates corrosion.
  • ✓ Expansive soils: West Jordan’s clay-heavy soil is prone to expansion and contraction with freeze-thaw cycles, snowmelt, and dry summers. The soil shifts and can stress underground pipe materials, leading to sagging, joint breaks, or cracks from compaction.
  • ✓ Freezing winters: Sub-freezing temperatures in the winter can freeze uninsulated pipes in exterior walls, garages, attics, and other unheated spaces.
  • ✓ Aging pipes: Older homes in West Jordan may still have original pipes that are at the end of their lifespans or outdated materials. These plumbing systems have a higher risk of leaks due to significant corrosion on galvanized steel or brittle polybutylene pipes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can hard water cause pipes to leak?
Yes, hard water can cause pipes to leak over time. Sourced from the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District, West Jordan’s water contains high levels of calcium and magnesium. These minerals react with metal pipes, such as galvanized steel in West Jordan’s older neighborhoods, resulting in corrosion and eventual thinning and pitting.
How do I know if I have a slab leak?
To know if you have a slab leak, turn off all of the taps in your home and check your water meter’s reading. Either look if the leak detection dial is moving or check back after 30 minutes for an increased reading. Other signs include low water pressure, cracks in basement flooring or drywall, and musty odors.
What’s causing my water bill to suddenly increase?
Your water bill may suddenly increase due to several potential problems, such as a hidden plumbing leak, a running toilet, or a broken irrigation system. Normal increases in water usage, such as houseguests, watering gardens, and filling pools, can also cause other spikes.
