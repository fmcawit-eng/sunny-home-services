Signs You Need Leak Detection

Hidden leaks rarely announce themselves, so knowing the warning signs helps you call for leak detection on the Wasatch Front before minor seepage turns into major damage.

On the Wasatch Front homes, subtle clues like a warm spot on the floor, a musty smell, or a water bill that keeps climbing are often the first signs of a leak you cannot see. The sooner it is found, the smaller the repair and the less damage to your home.

Call for leak detection when:
  • Your water bill jumps with no change in usage.
  • You hear running water when no fixtures are on.
  • There are damp spots, stains, or bubbling paint on walls.
  • A warm spot appears on the floor over a slab.
  • You smell must or mildew without a visible source.
Act quickly if you notice:
  • The water meter moving with everything shut off.
  • Pooling water or soggy areas in the yard.
  • Low water pressure with no clear cause.
  • Cracked or lifting flooring near plumbing.
  • Mold returning after you clean it up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you find a leak without tearing up my home?
Sunny Home Services uses non-invasive tools like acoustic listening equipment, moisture meters, and pressure testing to pinpoint a leak’s exact location. That means we can find leaks inside walls or under a slab without opening up large areas, keeping repairs targeted and demolition to a minimum.
Could a high water bill mean I have a hidden leak?
Often, yes. A water bill that climbs without a change in your usage is one of the most common signs of a hidden leak. We can check your meter, test the system, and locate the source so the leak is found and repaired before it causes further damage.
Do winter freezes really cause hidden leaks on the Wasatch Front?
Yes. Hard freezes along the Wasatch Front can crack pipes in walls, crawlspaces, and outdoor lines. The damage often stays hidden until a thaw, when water begins to seep. If you suspect a frozen or burst pipe, prompt leak detection helps limit the damage.
