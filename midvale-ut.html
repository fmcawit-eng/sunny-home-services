Frequently Asked Questions

Will a new HVAC system lower my energy bills?
A new HVAC system can significantly lower your energy bills. New, modern systems feature variable-speed motors, advanced filtration, and high SEER2 and AFUE ratings to deliver maximum energy efficiency and performance.
What size HVAC unit do I need for my home?
The size of the HVAC unit you need depends on several factors. Choosing the right size involves determining your home’s total square footage, layout, insulation, and overall household usage. A professional evaluation is recommended to ensure that you receive the right size for your needs.
Do I need a permit for new HVAC installation in my home?
Yes, Midvale requires building and mechanical permits for all new furnace and HVAC installations and replacements. These permits help to ensure that your new system meets all local safety codes and manufacturer requirements for safe, seamless installation.
