What Is a Whole House Air Purifier?

A whole house air purifier is a filtration or treatment system built into your ductwork that cleans the air each time your furnace or AC runs. Unlike a portable unit, a whole-home air purifier system reaches every space your ducts serve. The EPA reports that people spend about 90 percent of their time indoors, where some pollutants can reach concentrations several times higher than outdoors.

You can add an air purifier to most existing HVAC systems. Forced-air setups across West Jordan can usually accept a media cabinet, an electronic cleaner, or a UV light, depending on duct size, available space, and blower capacity.

Why Wasatch Front Homes Need Whole House Air Purification

Whole-house air purification matters along the Wasatch Front. Our valley geography, dry climate, and busy wildfire seasons all push particles indoors.

  • ✓ Winter inversions: During winter inversions in Salt Lake City, stagnant air can trap fine-particle pollution.
  • ✓ Wildfire season: Late-summer smoke drifts in from fires across the West and lingers in the valley.
  • ✓ Dust: Utah’s arid climate and steady new construction on the valley’s west side kick up fine dust year-round.
  • ✓ Tree pollen: Spring pollen rides in on the wind, on clothing, and on pets.

A whole-house air purifier can help with wildfire smoke and the smoke smell, though results depend on the system. A high-efficiency filter, ideally MERV 13 or higher, can capture fine smoke particles.

An activated carbon filter may also help reduce some smoke odors. A UV light air purifier does not capture smoke particles or remove smoke odors, so filtration is the primary option for smoke-related concerns.

Types of Whole Home Air Purifier Systems We Install in West Jordan, UT and the Wasatch Front

We install three main types of whole home air purifier systems.

High-efficiency media filtration

Media filtration is the go-to choice for dust, pollen, pet dander, and smoke particles. A deep-pleated media cabinet provides more filter surface area than many standard one-inch filters. Some media filters include activated carbon, which can help reduce certain cooking, pet, and smoke odors.

Electronic air cleaners

Electronic air cleaners use an electrical charge to capture particles, including very small ones. Their effectiveness depends on the model and maintenance requirements, so they should be evaluated carefully. The collector cells need regular washing, so they suit homeowners who don’t mind a little routine maintenance.

UV light air purifiers

A UV light air purifier uses UV-C light to target certain microorganisms. Properly designed systems can help limit biological growth on HVAC surfaces, such as evaporator coils, although typical residential units have limited effectiveness against airborne microorganisms. Coil-mounted UV lights can help limit microbial growth on damp HVAC surfaces, while in-duct systems expose passing air to UV light.

Which System Is Right for Your Home?

The right system depends on your household’s biggest concerns, your ductwork, and your budget.

Allergies, pets, and asthma

A whole-house air purifier with suitable filtration can reduce airborne particles, such as pollen and pet dander, which may help some people with allergies or asthma. Regular cleaning and controlling pollution sources remain important. For Sandy homeowners with dogs and cats, high-efficiency filtration may help reduce airborne pet dander.

Installation cost

Whole house air purifier installation cost depends on the system type, your ductwork, and the size of your home. Sunny Home Services will provide an honest assessment that best suits your home, family needs, and budget. You see the flat-rate price before any work starts.

Energy bills and airflow

The effect on your energy bill depends on the purifier and how often your HVAC fan runs. A properly sized filter should provide suitable airflow, but an incompatible high-efficiency filter can increase resistance and strain the system. For South Jordan homes with longer duct runs, our technicians check static pressure so your blower isn’t strained.

Whole home humidifier

A whole home humidifier may be worth considering given Utah’s cold, dry winters. Heating outdoor air without adding moisture lowers its relative humidity. A whole home humidifier can complement a purifier by adding moisture during dry Utah winters. Maintaining appropriate humidity may improve comfort, but excessive humidity can encourage mold growth.

Whole House Air Purifier Seasonal Maintenance & Tune-Up in West Jordan & the Wasatch Front

Filter replacement intervals vary by model, household conditions, and air quality. Some filters last several months or longer. UV bulbs commonly have a manufacturer-specified replacement interval, typically every one to two years.

  • ✓ Spring: Inspect the system after pollen season and before AC season.
  • ✓ Late summer: Check the filter before wildfire season and replace it if needed.
  • ✓ Fall: Replace the UV bulb if it has reached the manufacturer’s recommended service interval, and clean electronic collector cells according to the manufacturer’s instructions.
  • ✓ Winter: Check filter loading during inversions, especially in homes with pets.

Follow your equipment manufacturer’s maintenance schedule, and check filters more frequently during wildfire season or periods of heavy dust.
