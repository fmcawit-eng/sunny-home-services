Signs your AC needs repair

Common AC repairs across the Salt Lake Valley include:

Why Regular Maintenance Matters in Riverton’s Climate

In Riverton, where summer highs sit in the high 90s during the day and drop sharply at night, AC systems run long and restart often because of the wide temperature swings. Frequent startups wear down components, and if one part is failing, it can put undue stress on others, causing them to overwork and fail sooner.

Refrigerant charge should be checked annually. If it’s low, your AC may take longer to cool, and that shows up on your energy bill.

Annual AC coil cleaning is also highly recommended for Riverton homes. Construction and open land around the city produce excess dust in the air throughout the summer season. This dust settles on the outdoor condenser coil and blocks it, making the compressor run longer and work harder to move the same load.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a larger home need more than one AC system?
Sometimes a larger home needs more than one AC system. Riverton has many big floor plans on big lots, and a single unit can struggle to cool a sprawling or multi-level home evenly. Zoning, a second system, or a mini-split for the problem rooms are good options.
When is the best time of year to replace an AC unit?
If you can choose and plan ahead, the best times to replace your AC unit are spring and fall. Scheduling is easier outside peak season, and you avoid going without cooling in the heat of summer while the work is completed.
Do I need new ductwork when I replace my air conditioner?
In many cases, you won’t need new ductwork when you replace your AC, but we’ll inspect it anyway. Many Riverton homes built in the 2000s have ducts that are structurally intact, but leaky at the joints. Sealing them costs less than replacing them.
