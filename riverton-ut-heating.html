Furnace Repair Services in Riverton, UT

Our furnace repair services encompass every part and situation that can go wrong with your home furnace. Below, let’s look at the signs your furnace needs repair, common repairs, and our emergency dispatch service.

Signs your furnace needs repair

Look out for these signs that your furnace needs repairs:

  • Weak airflow coming from your vents.
  • Cold air coming from the unit.
  • Dirty air coming from vents, affecting your indoor air quality.
  • A strange burning or sulfur smell coming from the unit.
  • Frequent short cycling.
  • Loud noises, like banging, grinding, and hissing.
  • Yellow burner flame.
  • Rising heat bills with no change to your heating habits.

Common furnace repairs we perform

As a top furnace technician in Riverton, we perform these common furnace repairs:

  • Swapping out dirty air filters.
  • Replacing dirty flame sensors.
  • Duct cleaning.
  • Fixing faulty ignitor switches and pilot lights.
  • Fixing broken limit switches (to prevent overheating).

Emergency heating repair

We also offer emergency heating repair in Riverton. It helps to know the exact situations to call us for emergency services:

Heating Repair vs Replacement: How We Help You Decide

We are always asked by our customers if they should repair or replace their heating system. There are a few factors to consider before making your decision.

  • ✓ Age of the unit: After 15 to 20 years old, the efficiency decreases and frequent repairs are common.
  • ✓ Cracked heat exchangers: The heat exchanger converts the fuel or energy into heat and, for gas units, keeps carbon monoxide levels at bay.
  • ✓ The 50% rule: If you are facing repairs that equal half the cost of a new system, it is more cost-effective to replace it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does furnace repair cost in Riverton?
The cost of a furnace repair in Riverton varies. A simple filter swap-out or thermostat calibration will cost less than, say, a blower motor fan swap-out.
Why is my furnace blowing cold air?
Your furnace is blowing cold air for a few reasons, usually because of a dirty air filter, an incorrect thermostat setting, or a pilot light/ignitor issue.
How long does furnace installation take?
All in all, a furnace installation will take four to eight hours. This depends on your home setup and if any electrical box or ductwork modifications are needed.
Will a new furnace lower my energy bills?
Yes, a new furnace with a higher AFUE (Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency) number than your older one will run more efficiently. This translates into lower energy bills.
