If you’re ready for a major upgrade to your home’s HVAC system, we can help by converting your swamp cooler setup to a central air conditioner.

Choosing the Right Size Unit for Your Home

Sizing an air conditioner for a space involves using a formula called the Manual J� load calculation. Your HVAC technician considers your home size, air sealing, insulation, sun exposure, entry points, and several other factors. The resulting number tells them how many British Thermal Units (BTUs) your system needs to cool the home efficiently and effectively. Many factors go into proper AC sizing, so this step should always be left to the pros.
