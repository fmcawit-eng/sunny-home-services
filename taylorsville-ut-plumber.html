What Counts as a Plumbing Emergency

In general, a plumbing emergency is any plumbing event that poses a significant risk to health and safety, may cause significant water damage, or dramatically impacts your home. Common examples of plumbing emergencies include sewer line breaks and sewage backups, major plumbing leaks, and frozen or burst pipes.

What to Do Before Our Plumbers Arrive

If you suspect a plumbing emergency in your home, the first thing to do is shut off your water at the main water valve if you can safely access the area. Never touch standing water in your home, as it may be contaminated and pose a significant health and safety risk. It’s also important to shut off electricity to the affected area to avoid the risk of fires, electrical shock, and damage to appliances.

How much does a plumbing service call cost in Taylorsville?
Taylorsville plumbing service costs vary depending on your home fixtures and appliances and the extent of repairs required. At Sunny Home Services, we’ll provide you with an upfront quote, and we also offer membership plans to help you save on maintenance and service calls.
Why does my water heater run out of hot water so fast?
If your water heater runs out of hot water quickly, it may be due to a broken heating element, sediment buildup, or thermostat issue. Our licensed plumbers will inspect your water heater, and offer repair or replacement recommendations based on the extent of the issue.
How do I know if I have a slab leak?
You may have a slab leak if you notice signs like a sudden drop in your water pressure, unexpectedly high water bills, or soft spots in your floor. Slab leaks can pose a significant risk of water damage and structural damage, so it’s important to call promptly for leak detection services.
