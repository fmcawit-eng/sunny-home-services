Why does my water heater run out of hot water so fast?
If your water heater runs out of hot water fast, sediment buildup in the tank is likely the cause. Minerals settle in the tank and take up space meant for hot water.
How do I know if I have a slab leak?
Warm spots on the floor, running water sounds, and a sudden jump in your water bill are the most common signs of a slab leak. If you notice any of these indicators, contact our team as soon as possible for inspection.
How often should I have my plumbing inspected?
We recommend annual plumbing inspections for most homes. Older Millcreek homes near large trees can also benefit from regular camera inspections to spot root intrusion or failing lines early.
