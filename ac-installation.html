Time for a New Air Conditioner?

If your AC is less than a decade old, repairing it usually makes sense. We generally recommend a replacement if your air conditioner:

  • Is 12-15+ years old
  • Often needs repairs
  • Doesn't cool your entire home
  • Uses R-22 refrigerant
  • Stops running efficiently

If repairs cost 50% or more of a new system - or energy bills spike with no change in usage - replacement can save you more.

Why Replacing Your AC Is a Good Idea

Utah's dry air strains cooling systems over time. Beyond avoiding repairs, a new AC brings:

  • Lower energy bills (high-SEER systems)
  • Improved comfort (Manual J load calculation)
  • Full coverage (manufacturer warranty + workmanship guarantee)

We offer financing, so your replacement is covered with low monthly payments. Discounts, rebates, and tax credits may also be available.
