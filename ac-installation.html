Split Systems

A split AC system consists of two primary components: an indoor unit that contains the evaporator coil, fan, and air filter, and an outdoor unit that contains the compressor, condenser coil, and fan. Split systems can be configured as a traditional central air conditioner if your home has ductwork or as a ductless mini-split to cool garages, additions, and homes without ductwork.

Packaged Systems

Packaged AC systems contain all the key components in a single outdoor unit, eliminating the need for an indoor unit. These systems are ideal for homes with limited indoor space, such as multi-story buildings, mobile homes, and homes without a closet or basement space. However, the outdoor unit is larger than that of a split system because it must contain more components.

Ductless Mini-Split Systems

Ductless mini-split systems are dual-function heating and cooling systems that don't require existing ductwork. Instead, an air handler unit is installed in each room where you want temperature control, allowing you to create custom heating and cooling in every room. Because ductless mini-splits don't have the air loss associated with ductwork, they're often up to 30% more energy-efficient than central AC.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you add AC to my furnace?
Yes, if you have an existing forced air furnace system, we can typically install a central air conditioning system to add AC. One of our licensed HVAC technicians can perform a home inspection to confirm that your home is compatible and provide you with an estimate of the cost to add an AC unit to your furnace.
How much does AC installation cost on the Wasatch Front?
AC installation costs on the Wasatch Front vary depending on your home's size and the type of AC you'd like installed. Before we begin any installations, we'll provide you with a comprehensive quote so you can find the AC installation that fits your budget.
What size AC do I need for my home?
Choosing the right AC size for your home depends on your home's square footage, insulation, and your cooling preferences. You should always call a licensed HVAC technician to perform a home inspection and provide AC recommendations, as selecting an AC based solely on square footage can often lead to improper sizing and negative outcomes, such as AC short cycling and insufficient cooling.
What warranty comes with a new AC system?
AC system warranties can vary depending on the brand you purchase. In most cases, manufacturer warranties are valid for 5 to 10 years.
