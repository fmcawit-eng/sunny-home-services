What to Do Before Our Plumbers Arrive

If you’ve contacted our emergency plumbers, there are a few steps to take to ensure your safety and mitigate the damage until we can get there. Here’s what to do while you wait for one of our plumbers to get to the scene:

1
If you’re dealing with a leak, shut off the main water valve.
2
Turn off your water heater.
3
If there’s any chance of water coming into contact with electricity, shut off the power at the breaker panel.
4
Contain water using buckets or towels.
5
Keep kids and pets away from any sewage or leaking water.
6
Move belongings away from areas your plumber will need to access.
7
Take photos of any damage to help with your insurance claim later.
