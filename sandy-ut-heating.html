Signs your furnace needs repair

Look for these signs that may indicate your furnace needs repair:

  • Your furnace is blowing cold air due to restricted airflow.
  • The system produces strange, loud noises.
  • Some rooms are warm, while others feel cold or lukewarm.
  • The system keeps turning on and off frequently.
  • There are visible leaks or damage on or around the heating equipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I schedule heating maintenance?
You should schedule heating system maintenance once per year, ideally in early fall before the winter season hits. If you have an older system, we do recommend another follow-up maintenance service when the heating season ends.
Do I need a permit for a new furnace installation?
Yes, a mechanical permit is required for all new furnace installations and replacements in Sandy. These permits help ensure that all of the venting, electrical hookups, and gas lines comply with current safety and building codes.
How do I know if my furnace is a carbon monoxide risk?
A strong rotten egg odor or a flickering, yellow or orange pilot light are common signs of a potential carbon monoxide risk. Another serious risk to be aware of is if your furnace has a damaged or cracked heat exchanger.
