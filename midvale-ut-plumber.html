Water heaters

For homes that require an unlimited supply of on-demand hot water, tankless water heaters can be installed. These systems are highly efficient, but require maintenance and the odd repair.

Leak detection

We can confirm a suspected leak in your home plumbing system using advanced technology, like thermal and acoustic sensing, to find even the smallest hidden leaks and repair them.

Sewer line

If your home has a failing sewer line, you’ll most likely experience foul odors, backups, and multiple slow drains. Our plumbers repair or replace sewer lines based on local regulations. We also use trenchless techniques to preserve the condition of your yard or driveway. More information on trenchless repairs below.

Hydro jetting

When a drain clog is caused by tree root intrusion (common due to numerous old-growth trees in Midvale) or significant grease and debris buildup, hydro jetting could be the solution. By blasting high-pressure water down your pipes, we cut through the debris and scour the pipe walls.

Whole home water filtration

Midvale’s hard water quality is full of minerals like calcium and magnesium that cause buildup in pipes and appliances, and can leave water with a metallic taste and cloudy texture. Our plumbers can install and service whole home filtration systems and water softeners to improve the quality and taste of your home’s water.

Plumbing fixture & faucet repair

When your home’s water fixtures and faucets are leaking, Sunny Home Services provides fixture and faucet repairs. We also handle all garbage disposal needs.

Camera inspections

Our camera inspection services shine a light on the quality of your interior piping to find the source of blockages and other plumbing issues, and determine if hydro jetting is the right solution. Routine camera inspections also catch early signs of root intrusion and pipe misalignments. This is part of maintenance tune-ups, which we recommend scheduling yearly.

Trenchless sewer line repairs

Trenchless sewer line repairs mean feeding a new pipe into an old one (relining) or using a bursting head (pipe bursting) to break up old, damaged piping and replacing it with new pipe. Both of these trenchless methods only require small holes without digging up your yard or driveway. Sunny Home Services will always opt for trenchless methods when your home plumbing structure allows for it.

What Counts as a Plumbing Emergency

The following are situations to call an emergency plumber:

  • ✓ Unexpectedly, there is no water in your home.
  • ✓ Burst pipes causing gallons of water to damage your space.

What to Do Before Our Plumbers Arrive

Follow these tips to get ready for the licensed plumber’s arrival: turn off the water supply in the event of a pipe burst, clean up the area with towels (if safe to do so), avoid all signs of raw sewage and wait for the plumber, and leave the premises if you suspect a gas leak.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a plumbing service call cost in Midvale?
Plumbing service call cost in Midvale depends on the plumbing service needed. For smaller jobs like a simple faucet swap-out, budget around $200, while more involved jobs like leak detection are higher in cost.
Why does my water heater run out of hot water so fast?
Your water heater is running out of hot water fast due to a tank that is too small for your home usage rate. One solution is upgrading to a tankless water heater for unlimited on-demand hot water.
How do I know if I have a slab leak?
You might have a slab leak if you hear running water while all taps and water sources are turned off. You can also check your water meter for any movement.
