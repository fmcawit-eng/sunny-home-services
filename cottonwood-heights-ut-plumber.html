Common Plumbing Problems We See in Cottonwood Heights Homes

There are several common plumbing problems we see in Cottonwood Heights:

  • ✓ Hard water buildup: Hard water sources across the Wasatch Front create scaling in water heaters, pipes, and fixtures, which can accelerate wear, promote corrosion, and cause clogs.
  • ✓ Freezing and burst pipes: During harsh winter temperatures in Cottonwood Heights, uninsulated pipes in basements, garages, and attics can freeze and burst.
  • ✓ Aging pipes: Pre-1990 properties with original plumbing may have galvanized steel pipes with corrosion and pinhole leaks. Many homes also have polybutylene (Poly-b) pipes that have become brittle and may suddenly fail.
  • ✓ Broken sewer lines: Moisture-seeking tree roots, shifting clay soil, and other factors can damage underground sewer lines, increasing the risk of leaks, clogs, and backups.
