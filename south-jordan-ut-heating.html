Signs your furnace needs repair

If you notice any of these signs from your furnace, it’s time to get in touch with a heating company near you for repairs:

  • Loud, unusual noises from the furnace
  • Yellow or orange pilot light
  • Extinguishing pilot light
  • Weak or inconsistent airflow
  • Short-cycling
  • Burning or sulfur odors
  • Rising energy bills with no change in usage

Furnace Replacement and Installation in South Jordan, UT

Furnaces have an average lifespan of ten to 15 years. After this point, your system will struggle to run efficiently and is more likely to break down when you need it most. We always recommend investing in a new furnace installation before this point to ensure a sudden breakdown doesn’t leave you out in the cold.

New furnace installation

Our team provides expert furnace replacement in South Jordan. We carefully calculate the right furnace size and ensure a seamless installation with proper connections to ductwork, power, gas, and your thermostat. Once we’re done, you’ll be able to enjoy a warm, comfortable home with much higher energy efficiency than your old system provided.

Heat pump installation

Heat pumps are a great way to reduce energy bills and provide both heating and cooling in a single system. These units are easy to install because of their mini-split design, which means there’s no need for ductwork. If you’re looking to augment your current HVAC setup, contact our team for quick, reliable heating installation near you.

Choosing the right size system for your home

Furnace sizing is a more complex process than you may realize. We help you find the right size unit based on your home’s square footage, air sealing, insulation, and other factors. This ensures the new system can meet your heating needs without being too large for your home, which would waste energy and shorten the furnace’s lifespan.

Heating Repair vs. Replacement: How We Help You Decide

We strive to repair heaters whenever possible, but at a certain point, all furnaces need to be replaced. You should replace your heater if it’s more than 15 years old, as most heaters become increasingly inefficient and unreliable at that point. We also strongly suggest replacing your unit if repairs will cost more than 50% of a new system’s price.
