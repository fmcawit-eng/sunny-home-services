Hard Water and Its Impact on West Jordan Plumbing

Our hard water is full of minerals, like magnesium and calcium, that constantly build up on fixtures and, over time, narrow the diameter of home piping systems, causing clogs.

This mineral buildup also accelerates wear and tear on important appliances like water heaters, which could require a new water heater installation. The minerals coat the bottom of water tanks, causing rumbling and popping noises and blocking burners. Having Sunny Home Services inspect your water heater is an important part of routine maintenance.

Hard water can also reduce the efficiency of dishwashers and laundry machines, leaving dishes spotty and laundry with residue. Hard water minerals also cause dry and itchy skin and hair, and create whitened, powdery stains around faucets. Finally, while hard water is not harmful, it can impart a metallic or chalky flavor to water.

Common Plumbing Issues in West Jordan Homes

Beyond hard water, West Jordan and Greater Wasatch Front homes experience other common plumbing issues that Sunny Home Services is an expert at resolving. Common issues for plumbing in West Jordan, Utah, include:

  • ✓ Sewer line root intrusions: Home plumbing lines near old-growth trees are susceptible to root intrusion, which occurs when tree roots grow into pipes through tiny holes or cracks. We resolve this issue with hydro jetting, which uses high-pressure water to cut through roots and clean scale from the pipe.
  • ✓ Clogs and slow drains: These can be caused by anything from grease in kitchen drains to hair in bathroom sinks to other debris in sewer drains.
  • ✓ Frozen and burst pipes: Our Utah winters are cold, and water in home pipes, especially exposed ones in crawl spaces and basements, can expand and burst.
  • ✓ Aging fixtures: Older homes may still have stock fixtures that are prone to leaks and sometimes have low water pressure.
  • ✓ Outdated piping materials: Older homes in West Jordan can still have galvanized steel piping, which naturally corrodes, reducing water pressure and causing leaks and clogs.

When working on stubborn clogs, we will use a camera inspection to find the source and check the condition of your piping system.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I worry about aging copper pipes?
Copper lasts 50 to 70 years, but if you've had multiple repairs for leaks or clogs, it may be time to replace it. A Sunny Home Services plumber will assess its condition for you.
How often should I have my plumbing inspected in a hard water area like West Jordan?
We recommend having your plumbing inspected once or twice a year in West Jordan. If you have consistently struggled with hard water, it's better to have it inspected twice a year through our maintenance package.
What should I do if a pipe freezes?
Shut off your water and never try to thaw the pipe yourself with an open flame. Always call Sunny Home Services to limit water damage and restore the piping.
What is a slab leak, and how do I know if I have one?
A slab leak is a leak beneath your home's foundation that can drive up your water bills and cause rot and cracks in your home's underlying structure. Common signs include hearing water when everything is off and visible cracks in your flooring or walls.
Can I install a tankless water heater in my existing home?
Usually, yes, you can install a tankless water heater for unlimited on-demand hot water. Depending on the model you choose, the process may require updating your gas line or electrical box capacity.
