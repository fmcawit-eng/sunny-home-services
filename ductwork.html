Signs Your Ductwork Needs Replacing in West Jordan, UT

When your ductwork leaks too much to seal, becomes too damaged to repair, or isn’t designed for your current HVAC system, it’s time for replacement. Some of the most common warning signs that your ducts need an upgrade include:

  • You have uneven temperatures from room to room
  • Your utility bills are rising without a change in usage
  • There’s little to no air coming from the registers
  • You hear whistling, rattling, or booming noises coming from the vents
  • It smells musty or dusty when your system turns on
  • There are visibly crushed, disconnected, or hanging ducts in your basement or crawlspace
  • Your furnace or AC short-cycles or runs nonstop

Importance of Professional Ductwork Services in West Jordan, UT Climate

West Jordan’s triple-digit summers and freezing winters make professional ductwork services essential for keeping your home comfortable and energy costs in check. If your ducts aren’t properly sealed, they can pull those contaminants straight into your home and diminish your indoor air quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does ductwork installation or repair cost in West Jordan?
The cost of ductwork services in West Jordan depends on the size of your home, the conditions of the runs, and the type of service required. Our team provides flat-rate quotes for every job and offers flexible financing options for larger projects.
Can leaky ductwork really affect my energy bills that much?
Leaky ductwork can cause your energy bills to rise by as much as 30%. If your energy costs are rising without reason, our team can inspect your ducts and measure airflow to find out why.
Will new, properly sized ductwork improve my AC or furnace’s performance?
Installing properly sized ductwork lets your equipment perform the way it was built to. New furnaces and ACs are designed to move a specific volume of air, and ductwork that isn’t properly sized costs you in efficiency and comfort.
