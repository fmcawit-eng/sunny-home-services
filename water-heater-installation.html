Signs It’s Time to Replace Your Water Heater

Before your unit fails completely, you’ll often notice a few warning signs that you need a plumber to replace your West Jordan water heater:

  • ✓ Rusty or discolored hot water: Discoloration in your hot water is often a sign of corrosion inside the tank that can’t be reversed with a repair.
  • ✓ Strange noises: Rumbling or popping sounds from your tank indicate the burner is working through a layer of hardened sediment on the tank floor.
  • ✓ Water around the base: When your tank starts leaking, replacement becomes the only option to protect your floors and prevent costly water damage.
  • ✓ Hot water that runs out faster than it used to: Heavy sediment buildup reduces the volume in your tank, making your 50-gallon unit perform more like a 40-gallon unit.
  • ✓ Constant repairs: More than two water heater repairs in a year often make replacement a more cost-effective option than another service call.
  • ✓ An aging system: Tank water heaters last between eight and 12 years, while tankless systems begin to fail around the 20-year mark.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a new water heater or just a repair?
If your water heater is under eight years old and has a failing thermostat, heating element, or thermocouple, repairs are often the better value. Replacing your unit is a better investment when it is over 10 years old, the tank is leaking, or repair costs exceed half the cost of a new unit.
Do I need a permit to replace my water heater?
Local water heater replacement requires a plumbing permit and municipal inspection in West Jordan. We pull the permit and coordinate the inspection for every installation, keeping your homeowner’s insurance and manufacturer warranty intact.
Should I install a water softener with my new water heater?
Most Salt Lake Valley homes benefit from a water softener to reduce the hardness of their water supply. Softened water extends the life of your water heater, appliances, and fixtures and reduces scale that forces your system to work harder.
What size water heater do I need for my home?
The right size water heater for your home depends on your household size and hot water demand. A 40-gallon unit is best for one to two people, a 50-gallon unit suits three to four, and a 75-gallon unit is recommended for five or more people. Our technicians calculate the right capacity for your home during the estimate so you’re never short.
