Camera inspections are for two things: to see the condition of your home piping system and discover issues, like deep blockages causing main water line problems. Sunny Home Services will always feed a camera down your pipe before determining if hydro jetting is the right call.

Traditional sewer line repair requires digging up your yard or driveway, leaving you with expensive repairs. Trenchless techniques only create small holes and repair piping through pipe bursting or pipe lining. It’s also much quicker than traditional methods.

What Counts as a Plumbing Emergency

Call our emergency plumbers in Riverton if you’re experiencing:

  • ✓ Burst pipes
  • ✓ Sewer backups
  • ✓ Complete loss of hot water
  • ✓ Gas line breaks and leaks (smell of rotten eggs)

Riverton has three unique considerations that can cause emergency plumbing situations, and we have some tips to avoid them.

  • ✓ Our winter freeze-thaw cycles can result in cracking and bursting pipes. Always insulate your exposed pipes.
  • ✓ Hard water quality accelerates wear and tear on pipes, water heaters, and appliances. To avoid this, install a water filtration system.
  • ✓ Our soil loves to shift during periods of heavy rain and drier conditions. Scheduling routine camera inspections is a way to see if your pipes have been negatively affected.

What to Do Before Our Plumbers Arrive

In the event of a plumbing emergency, always do the following, if safe to do so: shut off the water supply to prevent water damage, move any valuables and electronics away from the space, clean the ground with towels (only for water leaks), and leave the home if you suspect a gas leak.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a plumbing service call cost in Riverton?
The cost of a plumbing service call varies based on the needs of your plumbing service. Expect to pay around $150 to $300 for minor repairs and higher for more involved repairs.
Why does my water heater run out of hot water so fast?
Your water heater could be incorrectly sized for your hot water usage or need a sediment flush. Installing a tankless water heater, which provides endless hot water, could also be the solution.
How do I know if I have a slab leak?
If you turn off all water sources and still hear running water, it could be due to a slab leak. Also, check for any water staining on walls and warm spots on your flooring.
Is it normal for my water pressure to drop suddenly?
If your water pressure suddenly drops, it can signal a water leak, a blockage in your pipes, or significant sediment buildup that is narrowing your pipe’s diameter. Call Sunny Home Services to assess the cause and find a solution.
How often should I have my plumbing inspected?
We recommend scheduling plumbing inspections every two years for new homes and yearly for older homes, to catch any minor issues before they become costlier repairs down the road.
