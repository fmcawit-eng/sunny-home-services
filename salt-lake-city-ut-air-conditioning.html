Choosing the Right Size Unit for Your Home

HVAC professionals use the Manual J® load calculation to find the right-size AC for you, factoring in your home’s size, insulation, air sealing, doors and windows, and other factors. This calculation ensures the new unit is powerful enough to cool your space without being oversized, keeping energy bills low while boosting your home’s comfort.

Salt Lake City’s Climate Considerations for AC Service

Salt Lake City’s dry, high-elevation climate puts unique strains on HVAC systems. Extreme summer heat forces your unit to work harder, increasing costs and reducing home comfort. While the low ambient humidity means your AC doesn’t have to work as hard, it also means homeowners often have to improve indoor air quality in other ways, such as with whole-home humidifiers. When the temperatures swing (sometimes as much as 30°F in a single day), it can reduce system efficiency or cause short-cycling in unmaintained units.
