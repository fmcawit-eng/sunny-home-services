Signs Your Water Heater Needs Repair

Your water heater usually shows signs of trouble long before it fails. Watch for these signs:

  • ✓ Your hot water runs out faster than it used to, or never gets as hot as it did.
  • ✓ Rusty or discolored water starts running from your hot tap while the cold tap still runs clear.
  • ✓ The tank pops or rumbles while it’s heating as steam bubbles force their way through sediment buildup that’s hardened on the bottom of your tank.
  • ✓ Water pools around the base or drips from the pressure relief valve.
  • ✓ Your pilot light won’t stay lit.
  • ✓ Your water use hasn’t changed, but your gas or power bill has gone up.

Pooling water needs attention as a matter of urgency. A 50-gallon failure makes for an expensive and unplanned repair, and it can be avoided with a quick call to our emergency plumbing team rather than waiting overnight to see what happens.

Common Water Heater Repairs We Handle

Most hot water tank repair in West Jordan comes down to a handful of parts that require replacement.

If you have a gas heater and your pilot light won’t stay lit, there may be an issue with the thermocouple or a gas control valve. Burner assemblies soot up over time and stop heating cleanly. When the problem traces back past the unit, our licensed plumbers handle gas line repairs as well. If you have an electric heater and you’ve lost hot water entirely, it’s usually a failed heating element or thermostat, both of which are straightforward swaps.

At Sunny Home Services, our technicians also replace leaking pressure relief valves, worn anode rods, and broken dip tubes, and we flush tanks that have filled with sediment. A leaking tank usually means the steel has corroded through and may require a full water heater replacement.

How West Jordan’s Water Affects Water Heater Lifespan

West Jordan’s water is hard enough to measurably shorten how long your water heater lasts. The city’s 2025 Consumer Confidence Report puts total hardness at an average of 191 mg/L, with samples running as high as 381 mg/L, which is considered very hard water.

Calcium and magnesium stay dissolved in cold water and solidify when it heats. Here’s what that does inside your tank:

  • ✓ Gas tanks: Sediment settles as a crust on the bottom, between the burner and the water you’re trying to heat, costing you more to heat the same volume.
  • ✓ Electric tanks: Sediment coats the heating elements, which run hot and burn out early. Elements are a frequent replacement along the Wasatch Front.
  • ✓ Tankless units: Minerals build up inside the heat exchanger, restricting flow until the unit stops heating altogether.

Regular water heater maintenance helps reduce hard water damage in your tank. Occasional repairs can usually keep a unit running for its full lifetime if it’s well maintained.

Repair or Replace Water Heater? What Our Technicians Look At

You should replace your water heater when the tank itself is leaking, or the unit is past its useful life. In almost every other case, you’re better off repairing it. Our technicians look at the following factors:

What part has failed

Thermostats, elements, thermocouples, and valves are all repairs. A corroded tank is a replacement.

Age of the tank

Tank water heaters typically last eight to 12 years, and hard water pushes West Jordan units toward the shorter end. After 10 years, a major repair often buys you less time than it costs. Tankless units can last closer to 20 years, but only with regular descaling. Scale builds in the heat exchanger faster than it does in a tank, and the hard water accelerates the buildup.

Repair cost against replacement cost

If the repair is going to cost you a large share of what a new unit would cost, we’ll tell you that instead of selling you the repair.

What you need going forward

If you were running out of hot water before the tank ever failed, you may be better served by a correctly sized new water heater, or by going tankless.
