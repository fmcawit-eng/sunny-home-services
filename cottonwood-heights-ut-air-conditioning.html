Choosing the Right Size Unit for Your Home

Installing the right-sized unit is important for efficient cooling. An oversized unit cools too quickly, so it shuts off before pulling moisture from the air, leaving the house cool but damp. An undersized system runs constantly without catching up. We measure your square footage, ceiling heights, insulation, and window orientation before recommending the best system for your Cottonwood Heights home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does elevation affect what size AC unit I need?
Elevation can affect the size of the AC unit you need. Thinner air carries less heat, and a unit’s rated capacity will behave slightly differently on the bench than at the valley floor. We factor elevation into our measurements before giving you a quote.
Do cottonwood trees affect my air conditioner?
Yes, cottonwood trees affect your AC. Their seeds collect on the outdoor unit and your filters during the summer, restricting airflow when you need it most. Rinse the unit and check filters monthly throughout the season.
Why is cooling so uneven in my split-level home?
Cooling may be uneven in your split-level home because of ductwork issues. Return air is usually the culprit, since lower levels often have supply vents without enough return. Zoning or a mini-split AC can help solve this issue.
