What Are Ductless Mini Splits?

Ductless mini splits are heating and cooling systems that deliver conditioned air straight into a room without the use of ducts. They feature an outdoor compressor unit and an indoor air handler mounted on a wall or ceiling, connected by a small line that runs through your wall.

Many Wasatch Front homes are equipped with multiple indoor units to provide zoned control for room-by-room comfort and better efficiency. Each indoor unit cools and heats the space it serves, while letting you set the temperature independently in each zone.

Mini split vs. central AC

Mini splits differ from central ACs in their setup and operation. Central ACs push conditioned air through a network of ducts, making them ideal for homes with existing ductwork. Ductless mini splits skip the ducts entirely, which means fewer installation requirements and less wasted energy for homes without existing ductwork or those that want targeted heating and cooling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a ductless mini split last?
With proper maintenance, a ductless mini split can last 15 to 20 years. Routine filter changes and biannual tune-ups keep your system running efficiently and help it withstand Utah’s scorching summers and frigid winters.
Do mini splits offer efficient heating in cold Utah winters?
Yes, cold-climate mini splits offer reliable, efficient heating through cold Wasatch Front winters. Modern mini split heat pumps are designed to pull heat from outdoor air even in cold temperatures, making them a reliable solution even during Utah’s coldest winter weather.
Will a mini split lower my energy bills?
Replacing an older, less-efficient system with a modern mini split will often lower your energy bills. Mini splits avoid the energy lost through ductwork and let you heat or cool only the rooms you’re actually using, helping many Wasatch Front homeowners lower their utility costs.
