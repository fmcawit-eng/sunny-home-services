Quality Heating Service in the Salt Lake City Area

When you need an expert heating company, Sunny Home Services is here to help. You can count on our team for emergency heating repairs during a cold snap, expert installation, or preventive heater maintenance. Extreme temperatures and valley air pollution can strain your heating system, but our local knowledge and solutions ensure your heater lasts and is energy efficient.

Heating Installation

Is it your first time installing a furnace or heater? Or does your struggling system need to be replaced? Factoring in your home, budget, and energy goals, we’ll help you find the perfect system. We offer many makes and models from the top brands, specializing in:

Gas and Electric Furnaces

We install energy-efficient furnaces with an annual fuel utilization efficiency (AFUE) of up to 98%, meaning they turn up to 98% of the fuel used into heat. Our RMGA-certified technicians install gas furnaces if you have a natural gas connection. Electric furnaces are suited for smaller homes without gas service.

Heat Pumps

Modern air-source heat pumps perform well in Utah’s temperature extremes and semi-arid climate. A cold-climate heat pump is an effective year-round home comfort option. Replacing your HVAC with a single heating and cooling solution reduces energy usage, making it a great investment in even an older home.

Ductless Mini-Splits

Whether your home doesn’t have ductwork, it’s too costly or disruptive to install it, or you need heating or cooling for a single room or area, we can install a ductless mini-split system. Multiple indoor units are often installed in local homes for zoned, room-by-room comfort. These units mount to a wall or the ceiling and connect to a single outdoor compressor.

Every install starts with a Manual J® load calculation to ensure your heater isn’t oversized or undersized for your home.

Call Us for Furnace Repair Right Away

Call us for furnace repair right away if you notice the following signs:

  • ✓Your home doesn’t reach the right temperature.
  • ✓You smell gas, burning plastic, or a metallic odor.
  • ✓The furnace shuts down too quickly.
  • ✓The carbon monoxide alarm goes off.
  • ✓A yellow or orange pilot light.
  • ✓Combustion or electronic ignition failures.
  • ✓Banging, rattling, grinding, or screeching noises.
  • ✓An increase in energy bills with no change in usage.

Heating System Preventative Maintenance

The climate along the Wasatch Front puts a great deal of strain on heaters. We provide heating maintenance to help your furnace last through a long, chilly winter and reduce the effects of dry, dusty air.

Routine annual tune-ups help your heater run more efficiently, reduce the risk of mid-winter breakdowns, and include:

  • ✓Thorough equipment inspections.
  • ✓Cleaning burners, flame sensors, and blower motors.
  • ✓Combustion tests to ensure efficient ignition.
  • ✓Thermostat calibration and smart thermostat setup.
  • ✓Gas pressure and line inspection.
  • ✓Condensate drain check.
  • ✓Air filter inspection and replacement.
  • ✓Safety and limit switch testing.

Emergency Heating Repair

Winters here can be brutally cold, adding to the strain on your furnace. West Jordan’s 4,300-foot elevation and inversion events create conditions that can add to the strain, causing your furnace to break down unexpectedly.

Indoor temperatures can drop fast, causing dangerous problems in your home. That’s why Sunny Home Services is always prepared to dispatch a team for prompt heating service. Once you call, we send a technician to diagnose the problem. They arrive in a fully stocked truck, so most issues can be fixed on the spot.

Signs of an emergency include:

  • ✓No heat
  • ✓Strange smells
  • ✓Unusual noises
  • ✓Ice on the heater
