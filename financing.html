Frequently Asked Questions

What can I finance?
Financing is available on qualifying HVAC and plumbing installations and major repairs, including furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, and water heaters.
How do I apply?
You can apply online or have your technician help you apply on-site. It only takes a few minutes and you’ll get a quick decision.
Will financing affect the price of my service?
No. You’ll always get the same upfront, flat-rate pricing. Financing just gives you a flexible way to pay for it over time.
