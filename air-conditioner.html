What does an AC repair typically cost along the Wasatch Front?

How quickly can you come out for emergency AC service?
We offer same-day responses for no-cool emergencies whenever possible, because we know a broken AC during a Utah summer cannot wait. If you wake up to discover your home without cooling, give our team a call as soon as possible to lock in the fastest arrival window.
Do I need a new AC or just a repair?
If your air conditioner is under 10 years old and the problem is minor, repairs are usually the quickest solution. But once your unit passes 12 years old and the damage is severe, replacement becomes a better long-term solution.
What should I do if my AC stops working?
If your AC stops working, start by checking the thermostat settings and filter to troubleshoot your system. If your cooling still does not start up, shut off the system to prevent further damage and give us a call. We will get the next available technician out to diagnose and repair the issue.
