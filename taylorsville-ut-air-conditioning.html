New central air conditioner installation

Central AC installation in Taylorsville cools your entire home by cycling refrigerant between an indoor evaporator coil and an outdoor condenser. These popular systems absorb heat and blow chilled air through your home’s ductwork for a cool, comfortable space.

Ductless mini-split installation

A ductless mini-split system provides outstanding energy savings without taking up much space, and no existing ductwork is required. We specialize in installing these popular systems, which are ideal for many older homes in Taylorsville that don’t have any ductwork, as well as smaller homes and new home additions.

Swamp cooler to central AC conversion

If your home has a swamp cooler and you’re ready to upgrade, contact us for professional conversion. This process involves removing the old unit, patching the opening, adding return air ducts, upgrading the electrical system, and installing the outdoor condenser and indoor evaporator. A swamp cooler-to-central AC conversion is worth the investment because it provides better temperature and humidity control for a more comfortable space.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does air conditioner repair cost in Taylorsville, UT?
AC repair costs vary in Taylorsville, UT, based on several factors. The complexity of the job, current demand, and whether the repairs are performed after hours may impact your total cost.
Why is my AC running but not cooling my home?
If your AC is running but it’s not cooling your home, it may be due to a dirty or blocked air filter or a frozen evaporator coil. Other causes may include a blocked outdoor condenser unit or incorrect thermostat settings.
Will a new AC system lower my energy bills?
Yes, a new AC system can lower your energy bills thanks to higher SEER ratings and new features. Variable-speed motors and advanced filtration can help your new air conditioner run more efficiently, helping you save on energy costs.
