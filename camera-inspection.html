A sewer camera inspection along the Wasatch Front is the fastest way to answer why a line keeps backing up, driven by the age of the home, local roots, and hard water buildup.

What a Camera Inspection Finds

A camera inspection either confirms a simple buildup problem you can clean, or reveals a deeper issue in the pipe that needs a targeted repair. Knowing which one you’re dealing with saves money and prevents an unnecessary dig.

Along the Wasatch Front homes, a line that’s just coated with scale or grease can often be jetted clean. Cracks, offsets, and heavy root intrusion point to a repair, and the camera shows exactly where so the fix is precise.

A cleaning usually solves it when the camera shows:
  • Grease or hard water scale coating the pipe.
  • A soft clog with clean pipe behind it.
  • Light debris that flushes out with jetting.
  • Otherwise sound pipe in good condition.
A repair is the right call when the camera shows:
  • Roots intruding through pipe joints.
  • Cracked, offset, or collapsed sections.
  • A belly or low spot holding standing waste.
  • Corroded, deteriorating clay or cast iron pipe.
  • The same backup returning after cleaning.
