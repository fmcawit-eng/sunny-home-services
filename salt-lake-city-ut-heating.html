Signs your furnace needs repair

Keep an eye out for these common signs that your furnace needs support:

  • ✓ Furnace short cycling, or turning off before your home reaches the temperature on the thermostat
  • ✓ Bad furnace smells, including burning, rotten eggs odor, or musty smells
  • ✓ Unusual noises like screeching, banging, or clunking
  • ✓ Inconsistent temperatures
  • ✓ A pilot light that burns yellow or orange instead of blue, which can indicate a carbon monoxide risk
  • ✓ The carbon monoxide detector is going off
