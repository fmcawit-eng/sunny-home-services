Signs your AC needs repair

As a homeowner, be on the lookout for the following:

  • Weak or warm airflow coming from your vents.
  • Hissing or other loud noises coming from the unit.
  • Strange smells like burning or musty odors.
  • Short cycling (turning on and off without properly cooling).
  • An unexplained surge in your power bill.

Ductless mini-split installation

Older homes in West Valley that don’t have existing ductwork will benefit from a ductless mini-split installation. Swamp cooler to central AC conversion

Some homes in West Valley use evaporative air, also called swamp coolers, that use constant water to cool homes. While they use less electricity than traditional ACs, they also need constant maintenance, like cleaning the water reservoir, and sometimes fail when the air becomes humid. Switching to central AC will result in effective whole-home cooling.

Signs It’s Time to Replace Your AC Unit

When looking at whether it’s time for an AC unit replacement in West Valley, there are a few factors to consider.
