Signs Your System Needs to Be Replaced

Look for these signs that may indicate your HVAC system needs to be replaced:

  • ✓ Uneven heating or cooling
  • ✓ Loud, unusual sounds or unpleasant odors
  • ✓ Visible leaks or signs of physical damage
  • ✓ High energy bills with no change in usage
  • ✓ Aging systems that are 10 to 15 years old or older
  • ✓ A yellow or flickering pilot light instead of a blue, steady flame

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for new HVAC installation in my home?
Yes, West Valley requires mechanical permits for new HVAC installations and replacements. The permits verify that all electrical hookups, gas lines, and ventilation meet current safety and energy codes.
What size HVAC unit do I need for my home?
The size of the HVAC you need depends on several factors, including the layout and total square footage of your home. We also consider insulation, the direction your home faces, and general household usage.
What plumbing issues count as an emergency?
Major leaks and multiple drains clogging at the same time count as a plumbing emergency. Other problems that constitute an emergency include no water at all, a burst or leaking water heater, sewage backups, and overflowing toilets.
