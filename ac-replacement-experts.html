Fast, Dependable AC Replacement in the Wasatch Front

Needing an AC replacement is often unexpected, but it doesn’t have to feel overwhelming. Our experienced team keeps the process simple and stress-free, from helping you choose the right system to completing a smooth, professional installation.

Sunny Home Services is your go-to AC company in Northern Utah, whether you’re replacing your existing system or adding air conditioning to your home for the first time. We understand that a new AC system is a major investment, so our friendly, knowledgeable team will guide you through your options, answer your questions, and keep the entire process simple from the initial consultation through installation.

Time for a New Air Conditioner?

If your AC is less than a decade old, repairing it usually makes sense. We generally recommend a replacement if your air conditioner:

  • ✓Is 12 to 15+ years old
  • ✓Often needs repairs
  • ✓Doesn’t cool your entire home
  • ✓Uses R-22 refrigerant
  • ✓Stops running efficiently

If repairs cost 50% or more of a new system, or energy bills spike with no change in usage, replacement can save you more.

Why Replacing Your AC Is a Good Idea

Utah’s dry air strains cooling systems over time. Beyond avoiding repairs, a new AC brings:

  • ✓Lower energy bills (high-SEER systems)
  • ✓Improved comfort (Manual J load calculation)
  • ✓Full coverage (warranty and workmanship guarantee)

We offer financing, so your replacement is covered with low monthly payments. Discounts, rebates, and tax credits may also be available.
