Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my heater needs repair or replacement?
Whether your heating system needs repair or replacement depends on its age and the extent of the damage. If your heater is reaching the end of its expected lifespan, which is typically between 15 and 20 years old, and if the repairs are expected to cost more than 50% of the value of a new unit, it’s often more cost-efficient to consider heater replacement.
