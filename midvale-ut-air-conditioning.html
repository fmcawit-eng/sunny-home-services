Signs your AC needs repair

Call us if your system shows any of these issues:

  • Weak or warm airflow
  • System turns on and off every few minutes instead of running a full cycle (short cycling)
  • Grinding, buzzing, or rattling noises
  • Temperature isn’t reaching the thermostat setting
  • Ice along the refrigerant line or evaporator coil

Signs It’s Time to Replace Your AC Unit

There are a few signs it’s time to consider a full AC unit replacement in Midvale:

  • ✓ Repairs are becoming more frequent and expensive.
  • ✓ The unit is reaching the 12- to 15-year mark.
  • ✓ The unit uses R-22 refrigerant, which is no longer produced.
  • ✓ Your energy bills are climbing despite no change in usage.

The older and less efficient your current system, the bigger the impact on your energy bills. A modern, energy-efficient system can help lower your bill.

Why Regular Maintenance Matters in Midvale’s Climate

Regular maintenance in Midvale is important because our climate puts two specific types of strain on our equipment.

During summer heat waves, the system repeatedly starts as your home reheats between cycles, gradually wearing down components. When one part weakens, it strains the rest of the system. Midvale’s dry, dusty summers coat the outdoor unit’s condenser coil, and when it’s blocked, the compressor has to work harder and run hotter.

Regular maintenance identifies these issues, prolonging the life of your equipment and saving you on energy bills.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can an older Midvale home handle central air conditioning?
Older Midvale homes can handle central AC if there’s ductwork serving a forced-air furnace. Without it, a ductless mini-split may be preferable to opening finished walls to run new ductwork.
Will I need an electrical upgrade for a new AC system?
You may need an electrical upgrade for a new AC system if you live in an older property and the panel was sized for a far smaller load. Because we assess capacity and the existing circuit at the quoting stage, we price any upgrade from the start.
Is it worth adding air conditioning to a rental property?
Many Midvale rentals lack AC, and it’s often worth adding for the comfort of the occupants. Where no ductwork exists, a mini-split is usually the most effective option for year-round comfort in a rental property.
