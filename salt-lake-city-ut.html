Signs Your System Needs To Be Replaced

Upgrading your HVAC system can lower your energy bills thanks to features like variable-speed motors and higher SEER2 and AFUE ratings. Here are some signs that your current system may need to be replaced:

  • ✓Your current system is around 15 years old or older.
  • ✓The pilot light on your gas furnace is yellow instead of blue.
  • ✓The HVAC system produces musty or rotten egg smells.
  • ✓Visible leaks or water are pooling around the unit.
  • ✓Your equipment starts to make loud, strange noises.
  • ✓Your HVAC system can no longer maintain consistent temperatures.

Do I need a permit for new HVAC installation in my home?
Yes, homeowners in Salt Lake City must have a mechanical permit for new residential HVAC system installations. They must also obtain a permit for HVAC system replacements to ensure that it meets all local safety codes and manufacturer requirements.
What size HVAC unit do I need for my home?
The right size HVAC system for your home is based on factors like the home’s layout, household usage, and overall square footage. Other factors that determine sizing include insulation, the number of windows you have, and the direction your home faces.
