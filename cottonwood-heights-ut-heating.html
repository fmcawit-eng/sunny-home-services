Signs your furnace needs repair

Furnaces give off warning signs before failing. Look out for these tell-tale signs that you need furnace repairs:

  • Loud noises (banging, grinding, hissing).
  • Strange smells (burning means electrical failure, and musty ones mean mold).
  • Dirty air coming from vents, negatively affecting your indoor air quality.
  • Yellow pilot light flame on the ignition system, meaning carbon monoxide gas.
  • Uneven heating between rooms.
  • Cold air is blowing from the furnace.
  • Spikes in your heating bills.
  • Short cycling: your system turns off and on before completing a full cycle.
Common furnace repairs we perform

Our local heating contractors regularly repair:

  • Faulty ignitors and pilot light controls.
  • Malfunctioning thermostats.
  • Clogged air filters.
  • Worn-out blower motors.
  • Ductwork leaks.
Emergency heating repair

Whenever you have a furnace emergency, our technicians will prioritize your service call. Emergency heating repair in Cottonwood Heights includes the following:

  • No heat during a snowstorm or below-zero day or night.
  • Smoke or flames coming from the unit.

Heating Repair vs. Replacement: How We Help You Decide

As a heating company near you, Sunny Home Services has three main factors for you to always consider when deciding on whether to repair or replace your heating system.

  • ✓ Multiple needed repairs: If your system has continued to break down after multiple repairs in the last few years, it may be time for a new one.
  • ✓ Age of unit: Older units (15 to 20 years) will always run inefficiently, driving up your energy bills, and break down.
  • ✓ Cracked heat exchangers: The heat exchanger converts fuel into heat and keeps harmful carbon monoxide away from your breathing air. It is quite expensive to replace, so it’s usually more cost-effective to replace the entire unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does furnace repair cost in Cottonwood Heights?
Furnace repair costs in Cottonwood Heights vary based on the repair needed. Simple jobs like a thermostat recalibration will cost less than a full blower motor fan replacement.
Why is my furnace blowing cold air?
Your furnace is blowing cold air due to dirty air filters, a failed ignitor, or a blower motor fan issue. Sunny Home Services will assess the cause and find a solution.
How long does furnace installation take?
A normal furnace installation takes between four and eight hours. For more complex jobs that require electric box or ductwork modifications, this number can increase.
How do I know if my furnace is a carbon monoxide risk?
If you have a gas furnace or fireplace, you should have a carbon monoxide alarm that will sound off. Another way to know is if your pilot light is yellow instead of a healthy blue.
